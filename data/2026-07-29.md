### [1] [Type Safety via Hoare Logic with Separation and Pure Types](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25262)
*Wenhua Li,Darius Foo,Quang Trung Ta,Wei-Ngan Chin*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出一个基于Floyd-Hoare逻辑的统一类型安全验证框架，通过四种核心成分（路径敏感的类型规范、分离类型、Err与Abrt的区分、类型谓词）将GADTs、liquid types等不同扩展统一到一个布尔代数中，并通过可判定的空性测试实现子类型检查，最终用Lean证明其可靠性和自认证类型检查器。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统类型安全"well-typed programs cannot go wrong"的二元判定无法区分堆所有权、流敏感类型变化、可恢复错误与致命错误等关系性、状态性事实，且每个扩展各自为政，难以保证其组合后的性质。需要统一的逻辑基础来整合这些需求。

Method: 提出四成分框架：(i) 路径敏感的类型规范（case specifications）；(ii) 受分离逻辑启发的分离类型（separation types），处理流敏感类型变异和必须别名；(iii) 区分Err（运行时错误值）和Abrt（编译时错误），提出"well-typed programs must never abort"的精炼格言；(iv) 类型谓词（type predicates）表达数据结构不变量。所有成分统一在同一个布尔代数中，子类型归约为一个可判定的空性测试。

Result: 在Lean中形式化了Hoare规则并证明了可靠性；通过证明反射实现了自认证的类型检查器，并在基准测试集上进行了评估。该框架能同时涵盖GADTs和liquid types，支持从容忍Err的弱规范到消除Err的强规范。

Conclusion: Floyd-Hoare逻辑为类型安全验证提供了统一基础，使原本各自独立的类型系统扩展能够在一个统一逻辑中自然表达和组合，且无需SMT求解器即可实现可判定的类型检查。

Abstract: Type safety has traditionally rested on carefully crafted type systems, under the motto "well-typed programs cannot go wrong". Modern demands push type systems past this basic guarantee: toward memory safety (e.g., Rust), stronger data-structure invariants (e.g., GADTs), and broader typability (e.g., MLstruct). The motto absorbs each such property by enlarging the set of states deemed "wrong", but collapses them into one binary verdict: heap ownership, flow-sensitive changes to a variable's type, and the gap between a recoverable and a fatal error are relational, stateful facts about intermediate states that one verdict cannot tell apart. Worse, each demand typically brings its own extension, making it hard to say what each guarantees or how they combine.
  Floyd-Hoare logic supplies a unified foundation. We present a framework for type-safety verification built from four ingredients: (i) case specifications for path-sensitive typing; (ii) separation types, inspired by separation logic, for flow-sensitive type mutation and must-aliasing; (iii) a disciplined distinction between Err (runtime error values our types track) and Abrt (compile-time errors), yielding the refined motto well-typed programs must never abort; and (iv) type predicates for data-structure invariants. Since all four are ordinary types in one Boolean algebra rather than separate extensions, the framework subsumes both GADTs and liquid types within one type logic, spanning weak specifications that tolerate Err to strong ones that eliminate it. Subtyping reduces to one decidable emptiness test, so a single lightweight procedure serves the whole framework with no SMT oracle in its trusted base. We formalise the Hoare rules and prove soundness in a machine-checked Lean mechanisation; by proof reflection it yields a self-certifying type-checker, evaluated on a benchmark suite.

### [2] [The Best of Times, the Worst of Times: Moment-Based Analysis of Probabilistic Cost Structures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25361)
*Chenyu Zhou,Di Wang,Thomas Reps*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出一种针对包含max/min极值操作的概率程序代价矩（均值、方差等）的组合分析方法，通过分层代价表达式和代理分布（精确短时前缀+紧凑参数化尾部）实现近似计算，并提供了可调精度的误差界。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有矩分析方法只适用于可加性代价，但现实问题（如争用解决协议、量子中继器等待时间、fork-join计算完成时间）中代价通过max和min组合，这些操作的非线性导致矩传播失效（max(X,Y)的矩无法由X和Y的矩确定），而传播完整分布又计算不可行。

Method: 提出分层代价表达式表示程序代价结构，自底向上求解局部递推方程，为每个子问题构建代理分布（精确短时前缀+紧凑参数化尾部），并支持通过细化代理表示提高精度。

Result: 在工具DICKENS中实现该方法，并在三个问题（量子中继器等待时间、RFID冲突解决、fork-join计算完成时间）上进行了评估。

Conclusion: 该方法能有效计算包含max/min极值操作的概率程序代价的各阶矩，且可通过计算量换取精度。

Abstract: This paper studies how to compute the moments -- mean, variance, and beyond -- of the cost (e.g., running time) of certain probabilistic programs, in which local costs combine not only additively but also via the extremal operations $\max$ and $\min$. Such costs arise naturally -- for instance, the number of rounds of a contention-resolution protocol, the waiting time of a quantum repeater, and the completion time of a fork-join computation -- but fall outside the scope of moment-based analyses developed for additive costs. The difficulty is that $\max$ and $\min$ are nonlinear: the moments of $\max(X, Y)$ are not determined by those of $X$ and $Y$, so propagating moments alone fails. In contrast, propagating full distributions would suffice, but is computationally intractable.
  We present a compositional cost analysis for a family of probabilistic programs whose cost structure can be represented as a hierarchical cost expression. The analysis proceeds bottom-up through the hierarchical structure, solving local recurrence equations at each node and summarizing each subproblem with a surrogate distribution. Each surrogate consists of an exact short-time prefix and a compact parametric tail. Our approach computes the mean of the cost distribution with a sound error bound, and systematically lifts to second and higher moments. In addition, precision can be increased by refining the surrogate representation, trading additional computation for tighter bounds.
  We implemented our method in a tool, called DICKENS, and evaluated its capabilities on three problems: quantum repeater waiting times, RFID collision resolution, and completion times of fork-join computations.

### [3] [Towards Bottom-Up Enumeration in miniKanren via Pruning and Memoization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25373)
*Nikolai Kudasov*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出了两个miniKanren库组合子——prune和defrel/bank，将基于观察去重的自底向上枚举技术引入关系式编程环境，以提升程序合成效率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 将非关系型PBE合成器中成熟的自底向上枚举与观察去重技术引入关系式编程（miniKanren），解决传统深度优先搜索在合成任务中的效率瓶颈。

Method: 1) prune组合子：通过用户提供的键（如输入/输出行为）对答案流进行去重；2) defrel/bank组合子：基于规范新鲜变量对关系进行记忆化，自底向上构建去重答案流并在调用点重放；3) defrel/bank-w加权变体：为未成熟流附加可接受上界，在深度优先规范顺序遗漏紧凑解时恢复最佳优先枚举。

Result: 在算术和字符串合成的PBE基准测试中，defrel/bank在多数深层次目标上显著优于深度限制基线，但在规范深度优先顺序遗漏紧凑解的一小类任务上表现较差。

Conclusion: 将自底向上枚举与观察去重引入关系式编程是可行的且对多数深层合成任务有效，但规范顺序的选择对性能有重要影响，加权变体提供了缓解方案。

Abstract: We present two small library combinators on top of plain miniKanren, designed to bring bottom-up enumeration with observational deduplication, the standard tool in non-relational program-by-example (PBE) synthesizers, into the relational setting. The first combinator, prune, deduplicates an answer stream by a user-supplied key, typically the input/output behavior of the candidate. The second, defrel/bank, memoizes a relation against canonical fresh variables so that a single pruned answer stream is built bottom-up and replayed at every call site. We also discuss a weighted variant, defrel/bank-w, which attaches admissible upper bounds to immature streams to recover best-first enumeration in cases where the natural depth-first canonical order misses compact representatives. On a preliminary PBE benchmark of arithmetic and string synthesis targets, defrel/bank substantially outperforms the depth-bounded baseline on most deep targets, while losing on a small family where the canonical depth-first enumeration order misses compact representatives. We leave a broader empirical evaluation to an extended version of this paper.

### [4] [Demystifying Deep Learning Compiler Frontend Bugs: An LLM-Aided Empirical Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25651)
*Xinyi Yuan,Wei Chen,Jinyi Liu,Pengyu Chen,Jun Wei,Guoquan Wu,Jiaxin Zhu,Tao Huang*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文首次系统研究了深度学习编译器前端（TorchDynamo）中的缺陷（fBugs），构建了包含7大类15子类的分类体系，并提出了一种结合领域知识的LLM辅助方法，成功发现23个新缺陷（15个已确认）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 深度学习编译器前端的缺陷（fBugs）严重但缺乏系统研究，现有工作多聚焦底层API/算子或将编译器视为整体，本文旨在填补这一空白。

Method: 采用领域知识增强的LLM辅助方法，对TorchDynamo中的123个fBugs进行系统实证研究，构建根因分类体系，并利用LLM生成根因感知的测试用例检测新缺陷。

Result: 构建了7大类15子类的fBug分类体系，并利用LLM生成针对性测试用例，在最新版本中发现23个此前未知的fBugs（15个已确认），覆盖8个（子）类别。

Conclusion: 本文的方法论在检测和加固DLC前端方面有效，为DLC开发和测试提供了可操作的见解。

Abstract: Deep learning compilers (DLCs) are designed to translate deep learning programs into optimized, hardware-specific code. Typically, DLC frontends translate programs into graph-based intermediate representations (IRs) to enable optimizations. Defects introduced during this stage (termed \emph{fBug}s) are severe yet understudied, as prior work predominantly focuses on low-level APIs and operators or treats DLCs as monolithic entities.
  To bridge this gap, we conduct the first systematic empirical study of \emph{fBug}s in TorchDynamo, the default DLC frontend for PyTorch 2, the most popular DL framework. Leveraging a domain-knowledge-enhanced LLM-aided methodology, we analyze 123 \emph{fBug}s and construct a taxonomy comprising 7 root cause categories and 15 subcategories. Our findings provide actionable insights for DLC development and testing. Furthermore, we leverage the LLM to generate targeted, root cause-aware test cases to detect new bugs. We uncovered 23 previously unknown \emph{fBug}s in recent releases (15 confirmed) across eight (sub)categories, demonstrating the efficacy of our methodology in testing and hardening DLC frontends.
