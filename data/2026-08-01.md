### [1] [BMOA: Baseline-Mechanism-Outcome Attribution for Compiler-Induced Numerical Deviations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27270)
*Hailong Jiang,Emran Hossain,Feng Yu,Chunwei Xia,Mengfei Ren,Jianfeng Zhu,Qiang Guan*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出BMOA（基线-机制-结果归因）诊断框架，将编译器感知的数值正确性问题从简单的"通过/失败"不匹配判定分解为三个独立维度：比较基线与系统边界、证据支持的编译器机制、以及参考限定的精度结果。通过结合多种比较策略（严格浮点、变换局部、可复现性、跨编译器、高精度），并在证据不足时保留混合/模糊/未知归因，BMOA将原始的不匹配转化为可审计、证据有界的记录。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的编译器数值正确性测试工作流通常将浮点差异的含义、证据支持的编译器行为以及数值后果三个不同层面的问题混为一谈，简单地将测试结果判为"通过/失败"不匹配。这种简化掩盖了关键诊断信息，无法回答"差异意味着什么"、"编译器行为支持什么证据"以及"数值后果是什么"这三个核心问题。

Method: BMOA框架将诊断分解为三个维度：（1）基线选择与系统边界（比较关系），（2）证据支持的编译器机制，（3）参考限定的精度结果。框架组合了五类比较方式：操作级严格浮点比较、变换局部比较、可复现性比较、跨编译器比较、高精度参考比较，并在证据不足时保留混合、模糊和未知归因。每条记录保留输入、配置、数值指标及支持审计的工件。

Result: 在六个科学计算内核、确定性压力输入族和ARM64上受控Clang配置上评估。1,276条记录归因语料库和162个实例的受控机制矩阵表明：基线选择会改变诊断结论；编译器诱导的偏差并不一定意味着精度损失；在目标矩阵中，抵消误差和大动态范围暴露了最强的效应。

Conclusion: BMOA将原始的不匹配转化为明确、可审计、证据有界的记录。虽然其自身不是证明系统，但这些记录为未来编译器感知数值正确性的形式化规范和证明义务提供了经验基础。

Abstract: Formalizing compiler-aware numerical correctness requires distinguishing what an observed floating-point difference means, what compiler behavior the evidence supports, and what numerical consequence follows. Existing testing workflows often collapse these questions into a pass/fail mismatch. We introduce Baseline--Mechanism--Outcome Attribution (BMOA), a diagnostic framework that separates the comparison relation and system boundary, the evidence-supported compiler mechanism, and the reference-qualified accuracy outcome. BMOA combines operational strict floating-point, transformation-local, reproducibility, cross-compiler, and higher-precision comparisons, while preserving mixed, ambiguous, and unknown attributions when evidence is insufficient. Each record retains inputs, configurations, numerical metrics, and supporting artifacts for audit. We evaluate BMOA on six scientific-computing kernels, deterministic stress-input families, and controlled Clang configurations on ARM64. A 1,276-record attribution corpus and a 162-instance controlled mechanism matrix show that baseline choice changes diagnoses, compiler-induced deviation does not imply accuracy loss, and cancellation and large dynamic range expose the strongest effects within the targeted matrix. BMOA converts raw mismatches into explicit, auditable, evidence-bounded records. Although it is not itself a proof system, these records provide an empirical foundation for future formal specifications and proof obligations for compiler-aware numerical correctness.

### [2] [Formalization of security](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28551)
*Gilles Barthe*

Main category: cs.CR

TL;DR: 本文介绍了证明助手在安全领域的多种应用，包括系统安全、基于语言的安全、安全编译和密码学，并强调其还可用于支持安全认证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 证明助手常用于验证设计和实现是否满足预期的安全属性，此外还可用于支持安全认证，因此值得系统性地总结其在各安全方向的应用。

Method: 本章按应用领域分类讨论证明助手的使用：系统安全、基于语言的安全、安全编译以及密码学。

Result: 概述了证明助手在上述四个安全领域中已验证的典型应用场景及其价值。

Conclusion: 证明助手不仅用于安全属性的形式化验证，还能为安全认证提供有力支撑，在多个安全子领域均有重要应用。

Abstract: Proof assistants are often used to validate that designs and implementations meet their expected security properties. A further motivation for using proof assistants is to support certification. This chapter focuses on their applications to system security, language-based security, secure compilation, and cryptography.

</details>
