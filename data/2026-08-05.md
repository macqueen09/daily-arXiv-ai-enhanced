### [1] [GenOS: Compositional Certificates for Semantic Robustness in AI Code Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03588)
*Corrado Priami*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: GenOS 是一个概率操作语义框架，用于形式化 AI 编码智能体中组件（提示、契约、生成器、程序）的可替换性问题。它将工作流各层建模为马尔可夫核，并通过观察者相对等价关系证明，等价的组件替换不会改变下游等价闭包事件的概率分布。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: AI 编码智能体是随机工作流，微小的提示或规格变化可能改变程序行为分布，即使文本看起来同义。现有系统只评估正确性，缺乏一个组合性的准则来判断在完整智能体工作流中安全替换某个组件（提示、契约、生成器或程序）是否可行。

Method: 将工作流的每一层建模为马尔可夫核，每个接口携带观察者相对等价关系。证明等价兼容的核可下降到商类，且商化与分布扩展和顺序组合可交换。建立了工作流互模拟、声音验证下的守卫提交安全、全变差非扩张性以及将近似误差归因到各流水线层的可加鲁棒性界。通过可执行的插入排序审计（含自然语言改写、形式契约、六个程序、两个观察者和 121 个输入的枚举执行）实例化理论，并进行了 20,000 次随机有限核试验验证。

Result: 实验验证了等价提示产生相同的代码类和提交分布；对给原地契约分派 5% 概率的提示，突变观察者能区分它，同时下游距离保持在预测界限内。20,000 次随机有限核试验中没有任何精确或近似定律被违反。

Conclusion: GenOS 是模型参数化的：兼容性是可测试的可测性质，而非对语言模型行为的假设。该框架为安全地组合替换 AI 编码智能体中的组件提供了形式化基础。

Abstract: AI coding agents are stochastic workflows: prompts are interpreted, artifacts are sampled, validators produce observations, and orchestrators commit or repair. Small prompt or specification changes can therefore alter program-behavior distributions even when the texts appear synonymous. Existing systems evaluate correctness, but lack a compositional criterion for safely replacing a prompt, contract, generator, or program inside a complete agentic workflow. We introduce GenOS, a probabilistic operational semantics for this replacement problem. Each layer is modeled as a Markov kernel, and each interface carries an observer-relative equivalence. We prove that equivalence-compatible kernels descend to quotient classes and that quotienting commutes with distributional extension and sequential composition. Hence, equivalent prompts induce equal probabilities for all downstream equivalence-closed events, including verified commit. We also establish workflow bisimulation, guarded-commit safety under sound validation, total-variation non-expansiveness, and an additive robustness bound that attributes approximation error to individual pipeline layers. An executable insertion-sort audit instantiates the theory with natural-language paraphrases, a formal contract, six programs, two observers, and exhaustive execution on 121 inputs. Equivalent prompts yield identical code-class and commit distributions; a prompt assigning 5% probability to an in-place contract is distinguished by a mutation observer, while downstream distances remain within the predicted bound. Across 20,000 randomized finite-kernel trials, no exact or approximate law is violated. GenOS is model-parametric: compatibility is a measurable property to test, not an assumption about language-model behavior.

The strongest model produces correct artifacts in 94.8% of responses, achieves at least 1.05x speedup in 83.3%, and obtains a performance success on 93.3% of cases. Nevertheless, correct artifacts often close only part of the oracle gap. ### [4] [Certified Split Points for Parallel Lexing: Exact and Modulo Discarded Tokens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03473)
*Nicklas Nidhögg*

Main category: cs.FL

TL;DR: 本文提出了两种无需预扫描、推测或重叠即可并行DFA词法分析的条件：精确的"认证分割符号"（certified split symbol）条件和更宽松的近似条件。前者是充要条件，后者在丢弃声明的字符集后可提供保证。在munch库中实现了精确条件，八线程下达到92.6-95.3%并行效率，四线程下3.46-3.94x端到端加速。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的表驱动DFA词法分析是串行的，每次转移依赖前一字节的状态。并行扫描需要每个块的入口状态，现有方法通过模拟、推测、预扫描或重叠来恢复，这些方法开销大且复杂。作者希望找到无需这些额外机制的条件，使分块并行变得自然且高效。

Method: 提出了两个条件：(1) 精确条件——"认证分割符号"：当某个字节b使得除q0外无可达状态有b转移能到达接受态，且q0不可重入时，b的每次出现都必然开启一个token。该条件是充要的但脆弱（注释/空白常会消除证书）；(2) 放宽条件——将保证弱化为"删除声明的丢弃集合后相等"，从同一张表中判定，只需一次常量时间单比特查询，更宽松但保守（非精确）。该条件以查询形式提供，由调用者自行规划分块边界。

Result: 精确条件为常规C风格tokenization恢复了换行符，为JSON恢复了tab、换行和回车符（不改变token定义），但在块注释不受限时拒绝该条件。在munch库中，八线程下达到92.6-95.3%并行效率（512 MiB密集语料，超出最后一级缓存），四线程下3.46-3.94x端到端加速。

Conclusion: 该方法将基于分隔符的并行词法分析从"语言特定的假设"转变为"编译器可检查的性质"。精确条件虽好但脆弱，放宽条件提供了实用折中。该方法已在munch库中实现并验证了显著的并行加速效果。

Abstract: Table-driven DFA lexing is sequential: each transition depends on the previous byte's state. Scanning one input in parallel needs each chunk's entry state, which existing methods recover by simulation, speculation, prescanning, or overlap. We give two conditions under which none is needed. For a longest-match scanner restarting from q0 at every token boundary, a byte b is a certified split symbol when no reachable state other than q0 has a b-transition whose target can reach acceptance, and q0 is not re-entrant if it has one. Every occurrence of such a byte in completely tokenizable input begins a token, so chunks starting there reproduce the serial sequence of kinds and lengths by ordered concatenation. The condition is necessary as well as sufficient, and fragile: one string, comment, or whitespace run can eliminate every useful certificate, and comments and whitespace are usually discarded. We therefore weaken the guarantee to equality after deleting a declared discarded set, and give a second condition, sound and strictly more permissive but conservative rather than exact, decided from the same tables, answered by a second constant-time one-bit query. It recovers newline for a conventional C-like tokenization and tab, newline and carriage return for JSON, without altering their token definitions, and refuses it where block comments are unrestricted. It ships as a query only: the library's planner and every measurement here use the exact condition, so a caller must plan boundaries itself. Splitting at exact certificates in the munch library reaches 92.6-95.3% parallel efficiency at eight threads on a restricted CPU set, on a 512 MiB dense corpus beyond last-level cache, and a 3.46-3.94x end-to-end speedup at four threads, across two benchmark revisions on one machine. It turns delimiter-based parallel lexing from a language-specific assumption into a property a compiler checks.
