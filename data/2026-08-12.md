### [1] [Evaluating Shrinking (Experience Report)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09935)
*Alperen Keles,George Miao,Leonidas Lampropoulos*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文是一份经验报告，定量评估了三个Haskell属性测试框架（QuickCheck、Hedgehog、Falsify）的shrinking（收缩）性能，涵盖了四个ETNA工作负载和多种生成器家族，同时测量了有效性和成本两方面指标，发现QuickCheck的结构化收缩通常更快且最终反例质量具有竞争力，而集成式收缩并不自动保证优势。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 属性测试框架依赖shrinking将随机失败转化为可调试的反例，但现有研究主要衡量框架的bug发现能力，对shrinking本身的性能缺乏定量评估。作者希望通过系统的经验研究填补这一空白。

Method: 对三个Haskell框架（QuickCheck、Hedgehog、Falsify）在四个ETNA工作负载和多种生成器家族（基于类型、基于API、构造即正确）上进行对比。有效性通过树编辑距离衡量到穷举搜索所得最小值的差距；成本则通过收缩时间和每单位收缩进度耗时来衡量。

Result: QuickCheck的结构化收缩通常更快，且在最终反例质量上与其它框架保持竞争力；集成式收缩（integrated shrinking）本身并不能保证性能或有效性上的优势。

Conclusion: 这些发现对未来收缩算法的评估和设计具有重要意义，提示研究者不应假设集成式收缩一定优于结构化收缩，需要在多个维度上综合评估。

Abstract: Property-based testing frameworks rely on shrinking to turn noisy random failures into counterexamples that developers can debug. Although bug-finding performance is routinely measured, shrinking itself is rarely evaluated quantitatively. We present an experience report on evaluating shrinking across three Haskell frameworks: QuickCheck, Hedgehog, and Falsify. The comparison spans four ETNA workloads and several generator families, including type-based, API-based, and correct-by-construction generators. We measure both effectiveness, using tree edit distance to a ground-truth minimum found by exhaustive search, and cost, using shrink time and time per unit of shrinking progress. Across these workloads, QuickCheck's structural shrinking is usually faster and remains competitive on final counterexample quality; integrated shrinking does not by itself guarantee a performance or effectiveness advantage. We discuss what these results imply for future evaluations and designs of shrinking algorithms.

### [2] [Mixed Choice Multiparty Session Types, Precisely](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10704)
*Jake Masters,Nobuko Yoshida*

Main category: cs.LO

TL;DR: 本文针对混合选择多参与者会话类型（含会话委托、创建和交错）提出并证明了子类型关系的精确性（sound and complete），并通过引入三参与者锁和三方调度器进程构造技术，为完整类型系统建立了可靠性和完备性证明，同时提供了二次时间复杂度的子类型检查与安全性验证算法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 精确（sound且complete）的子类型关系能够保证类型安全的替换性质。然而，对于混合选择（mixed choice）的多方会话类型，尤其是包含会话委托、创建和交错的场景，子类型关系的精确性此前尚未被证明。此外，完备性证明需要一个通用的类型系统来支撑，这在混合选择多参与者会话π-演算中尚属空白。

Method: 1) 提出了第一个针对完整的混合选择多参与者会话π-演算的通用类型系统，用于证明子类型关系的可靠性（soundness）；2) 引入"三参与者锁"（three-party lock），作为处理交错会话的最简且通用的活性（liveness）错误形式；3) 建立了一种基于调度器进程（scheduler processes）构造的新证明技术，能够对所有子类型关系失败进行穷尽检测，用于证明完备性（completeness）；4) 将精确性结果扩展到一族混合选择多参与者会话类型。

Result: 1) 证明了混合选择多参与者会话类型（含委托、创建和交错）子类型关系的精确性；2) 实现了检查子类型关系以及安全性、死锁自由和活性的算法，优化至相对于状态空间和类型上下文大小的二次时间复杂度；3) 通过文献中的混合选择案例研究对算法进行了评估。

Conclusion: 本文首次为含混合选择的多方会话类型建立了子类型关系的精确性证明，填补了该领域的重要理论空白，并提供了高效可用的验证工具。

Abstract: A precise (sound and complete) subtyping relation $\leq$ specifies that $T'$ is a subtype of $T$ if and only if a program of type $T'$ can always safely replace a program of type $T$ without compromising the safety of a larger program. This paper formulates and proves preciseness of subtyping for mixed choice multiparty session types with session delegation, creation, and interleaving. We prove soundness by developing the first general type system for a full mixed choice multiparty session $π$-calculus. To prove completeness, we introduce the three-party lock, which is a minimal and general form of liveness error for handling interleaved sessions, and we establish a new proof technique based on a construction of scheduler processes which enable exhaustive detection for all failures of the subtyping relation. We then extend the preciseness results to a family of mixed choice multiparty session types. Algorithms for checking (1) subtyping and (2) safety, deadlock-freedom, and liveness of typing contexts are fully implemented and optimised to run in quadratic time with respect to the size of the state space and typing context, and have been evaluated with (mixed choice) case studies from the literature.

</details>

### [3] [Time to Move on: Querying without Nulls and Bags](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10863)
*Molham Aref,Leonid Libkin,Wim Martens*

Main category: cs.DB

TL;DR: 本文基于Rel语言的设计经验，论证了完全基于规范化关系（set semantics）的查询语言是可行的，并驳斥了SQL中bags（多重集）和nulls（空值）作为"不可避免之恶"的论点，指出这些设计决策是可以完全避免的。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: SQL以其声明性获得巨大成功，但随着查询语言需要解决的问题日益复杂，早期语言设计决策（引入bags和nulls）需要被重新审视。作者认为SQL社区将bags和nulls视为"不可避免的邪恶"，但这实际上是可以避免的。

Method: 作者基于Rel语言（一种端到端关系编程语言）的实际设计和部署经验，对支持bags和nulls的传统论点进行逐一驳斥，并展示基于完全规范化关系（set semantics）和无null的语言设计如何可行。

Result: 作者成功设计、实现并部署了基于完全规范化关系的Rel语言，证明了不依赖bags和nulls的关系语言是可以实现的。同时系统地反驳了为bags和nulls辩护的种种理由。

Conclusion: Bags和nulls并非查询语言设计中不可避免的组成部分，完全可以通过采用规范化关系和集合语义来避免，从而消除Codd所称的"损坏关系"和"十亿美元的错误"。

Abstract: SQL is the database community's success story in terms of language design. The key reason for its success is its declarativeness: it gives rise to optimizability, reducing the programmer's burden significantly. However, given the evolving complexity of problems to solve with query languages, our community needs to re-think some of the fundamental early query language design decisions. Our experience in having worked on the design of Rel (a language for end-to-end relational programming) tells us that it is possible to design, implement, and successfully deploy a language based on fully normalized relations. Such relations avoid what Codd called corrupted relations and what we commonly refer to as bags, and the 'harmful' 'billion dollar mistake' that we know as nulls. In the SQL world, it is accepted that bags and nulls are tolerated as an unavoidable evil. We argue that the evil is completely avoidable: reasons offered for justifying bags and nulls evaporate at a closer examination. In addition to debunking them, we also describe opportunities offered by a null-free language with set semantics.

### [4] [Comprendia: AI-Augmented Code Comprehension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10290)
*Costain Nachuma,Minhaz F. Zibran*

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: Comprendia 是一个 Eclipse 插件，结合结构依赖可视化和 LLM 代码解释，在共享交互图上支持 Java 程序理解。其核心创新包括图感知调用者剪枝（GACP）策略、克隆检测覆盖层和 CVE 风险覆盖层。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 程序理解需要同时利用结构化的代码依赖信息和 LLM 的自然语言解释能力，但现有工具通常将这两者割裂开，缺乏统一的交互界面和可审计的解释生成机制。

Method: 基于四大支柱构建：多边类型依赖图（支持实时搜索和多布局）、图感知调用者剪枝（GACP）策略的 LLM 解释、克隆检测覆盖层（提示提取到父类的重构机会）、以及基于 OSV.dev 的 CVE 风险覆盖层。GACP 利用图距离、继承折叠和边类型权重生成可复现且可追溯到图节点的提示。

Result: 在包含已知克隆和漏洞的 Java 项目上进行演示，展示了统一图基板如何支持理解并保持开发者的控制权。

Conclusion: 通过统一的图基板整合结构可视化与 LLM 解释，使解释可复现、可追踪，同时保留开发者对分析过程的控制权。

Abstract: Comprendia is an Eclipse plugin that integrates structural dependency visualization with LLM-powered code explanation on a shared interactive graph for Java program comprehension. The tool rests on four pillars: (1) a multi-edge-type dependency graph with live search and multiple layouts; (2) LLM explanations grounded in Graph-Aware Callee Pruning (GACP), an auditable strategy that selects relevant callees using the same graph the developer navigates; (3) a clone-detection overlay that highlights duplication and suggests extract-to-parent refactoring opportunities; and (4) a CVE risk overlay powered by OSV.dev. GACP uses graph distance, inheritance collapse, and edge-type weighting to produce prompts that are reproducible across LLM families and traceable to visible graph nodes. We demonstrate Comprendia on a Java project containing known clones and vulnerabilities, showing how the unified graph substrate supports comprehension while keeping the developer in control. ### [5] [Conflict Extraction in Probabilistic Datalog Analyses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10755)
*Siyu Chen,Chungha Sung,Xuyang Li,Jingbo Wang*

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: 本文提出PPProbe，一个专门针对概率Datalog分析的冲突提取器，通过利用Datalog推导图的结构来高效识别最小不可满足子集（MUSes），解决概率分析中边缘概率导致的警报间相互不一致问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 概率Datalog扩展使静态分析（如指针分析、数据竞争检测、侧信道分析）能够按可能性对警报排序，但引入了确定性分析中不存在的新挑战：最终输出可能包含单独看似合理但彼此不一致的警报，因为边缘概率不保证联合可满足性。开发者可能花费精力调查任何可能世界都永远不会同时出现的警报组合。

Method: PPProbe利用Datalog推导图的结构特性引导搜索走向可能的冲突，并通过自底向上的UNSAT推理来剪枝搜索空间。它不是泛化地改进MUS枚举，而是针对概率Datalog分析的特定结构进行优化。

Result: 在70个基准测试（涵盖功率侧信道分析、数据竞争检测、语义差异分析和贝叶斯网络推理）上，PPProbe比最先进的MUS枚举器实现了2.5到24倍的吞吐量提升，且识别出的冲突能保守估计减少误报，平均过滤掉47.7%的相互不一致警报。

Conclusion: PPProbe通过专门针对概率Datalog分析结构设计的冲突提取方法，有效解决了概率分析中警报间不一致性问题，大幅提升了效率并显著减少了开发者需要调查的无效警报。

Abstract: Probabilistic extensions of Datalog enable static analyses such as pointer analysis, data race detection, and side-channel analysis to rank alarms by likelihood, but this added expressiveness also introduces a new challenge absent from deterministic analyses: the final output may contain alarms that are individually plausible yet mutually inconsistent, because marginal probabilities do not guarantee joint satisfiability. As a result, developers may spend effort investigating combinations of alarms that can never co-occur in any possible world. We address this problem by formalizing such inconsistencies as minimal unsatisfiable subsets (MUSes) and introducing PPProbe, a conflict extractor specialized for probabilistic Datalog analyses. Rather than improving MUS enumeration in general, PPProbe exploits the structure of Datalog derivation graphs to guide the search toward likely conflicts and prune the search space through bottom-up UNSAT inference. We evaluate PPProbe on 70 benchmarks from power side-channel analysis, data race detection, semantic diffing, and Bayesian-network inference. The results show that PPProbe achieves 2.5 to 24 times higher throughput than state-of-the-art MUS enumerators, and that the conflicts it identifies yield a conservative estimate of false-positive reduction, filtering out an average of 47.7% of mutually inconsistent alarms.

</details>
