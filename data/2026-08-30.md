### [1] [Direct Manipulation and Natural Language Programming, Together at Last?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26359)
*Parker Ziegler,David Minh-Duy Cao,Justin Lubin,Sarah E. Chasins*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出一个统一的编程框架，将直接操作编程与自然语言编程相结合，以编辑语言（edit language）作为共享接口，并基于cartokit系统实现。通过18人受试者实验发现，当两种编辑方式同时可用时，用户绝大多数（~94%）选择直接操作而非自然语言编辑；直接操作有助于任务分解、增量编辑，并缓解自然语言编程中的模型理解问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 编程语言研究虽已提出文本编辑之外的多种编程方式（直接操作、结构编辑、自动重构），但自然语言编程的快速发展仍将程序视为文本、编辑视为非结构化文本变换。作者希望弥合不同编辑范式之间的鸿沟，探索如何构建支持多种编辑范式协同工作的统一编程系统。

Method: 核心洞察是将程序视为结构化编辑序列，以编辑语言作为直接操作和自然语言交互的共享接口，并利用约束解码（constrained decoding）支持自然语言编辑。在cartokit直接操作编程系统的基础上进行实例化实现，并通过受试者内实验（N=18）对比单一模态与组合模态下的编程过程。

Result: 在同时提供两种模态时，参与者仅6.14%的编辑通过自然语言完成，绝大多数选择直接操作。主题分析显示：直接操作有助于任务分解、促进增量编辑，并缓解了自然语言编程中理解模型能力及模型生成代码的已知挑战。该结果出乎研究者意料。

Conclusion: 基于编辑的框架和实验结果为未来融合自然语言与其他编辑范式的编程系统研究提供了可能的路径。直接操作与自然语言可以互补，直接操作在任务分解和代码理解方面具有优势，而自然语言编辑有潜力在合适的框架下发挥作用。

Abstract: Decades of programming languages research has contributed novel approaches to program editing that go beyond modifying text, including direct manipulation programming, structure editing, and automated refactoring tools. However, the rapid growth of natural language programming largely reinforces a view of programs as text and program editing as (unstructured) text transformation. How can we develop unified programming systems that bridge the gap between these approaches, supporting multiple editing paradigms in concert? We take a first step toward answering these questions by introducing a framework that enables program editing via both direct manipulation and natural language, and instantiate this framework in a variant of the $\texttt{cartokit}$ direct manipulation programming system. Our key insight is to treat programs as sequences of structured edits and to use an edit language as a shared interface for both direct manipulation and natural language interactions, leveraging constrained decoding to support the latter. Using our instantiation, we conducted a within-subjects study ($N$=18) to understand how the combination of direct manipulation and natural language as editing modalities changes the programming process compared to each modality alone. Perhaps surprisingly, we found that study participants overwhelmingly chose to edit via direct manipulation when both modalities were available, performing just 6.14% of edits via natural language. Our thematic analysis of study sessions revealed that direct manipulation aided task decomposition, encouraged incremental editing, and helped mitigate known challenges in natural language programming related to understanding model capabilities and model-generated code. Our edit-based framework and study findings lay out a possible pathway for future research on programming systems that blend natural language with alternative editing modalities.

### [3] [Tacet: A Language and Type System for Automatic Statistical Validity Accounting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27451)
*Chiké Abuah*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出 Tacet，一种用于统计比较的语言，通过类型系统确保实证研究中的统计声明的有效性。其核心演算 T 将自由估计子语言（携带足迹和纯度位）与定价声明子语言（携带财富变换器）连接，静态计算配对或聚类等比较结构，并通过反对性财富变换器实现预注册作为类型规则。语义在 Lean 4 中机器验证，并在 SWE-bench Verified 和 BIG-Bench Hard 上进行了案例研究。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 计算机科学研究中的系统间实证比较很少经过统计有效性检验，多数甚至从未被框架化为统计检验。现有的多重比较程序虽能控制错误，但需要关于分析检验了什么以及观测如何排列的输入，而这些无法从 p 值列表中恢复。这导致统计声明缺乏严谨性，需要一种机制来强制分析者声明其生成内容和期望，并拒绝无法承担或无法正确检验的声明。

Method: Tacet 是一种语言，其核心演算 T 由两部分组成：(1) 自由估计子语言，携带报告足迹和纯度位（记录是否在构建值时咨询了任何结果）；(2) 定价声明子语言，携带财富变换器。两者仅通过定价比较的机制连接。关键机制包括：读取结果选择样本会永久设置纯度位并记录其检查过的一切；配对或聚类的比较类型从工件模式中静态计算，仅依据键字段间的声明的函数依赖；假设该结构消失的机制会被拒绝而非定价；由于财富变换器对实现的 p 值具有反单调性，可负担性可在分析运行前检查，将预注册转化为类型规则。

Result: 作者在 Lean 4 中机器验证了元理论，无任何特设缺口。在参考实现和两个已发布工件案例研究（SWE-bench Verified 排行榜和 BIG-Bench Hard）上展示了该方法。

Conclusion: Tacet 通过类型系统机制将统计有效性检查内建于语言设计中，消除对分析者意图的依赖（无需询问是否故意摘樱桃），并能静态地确定比较结构、通过反单调性实现预注册。这为实证计算机科学研究中的统计严谨性提供了一种形式化的、可验证的方法。

Abstract: Empirical comparisons between systems are a standard form of evidence in computer science research, but few are checked for statistical validity: most are never framed as statistical tests at all. Existing multiple-comparison procedures could control the resulting error, but need inputs (what an analysis examined, and how its observations are arranged) that are not recoverable from a list of p-values.
  We introduce Tacet, a language in which an analysis declares what it generated, states what it expects to find, and is refused any claim it cannot afford or cannot properly test. Its core calculus T pairs a free estimation sublanguage, carrying a reported footprint and a purity bit that records whether any outcome was consulted in building a value, with a priced claim sublanguage, carrying a wealth transformer, connected only by a mechanism that prices a comparison. A sample selected by reading outcomes sets the purity bit and is recorded as having examined everything it read, permanently, so it can never be granted a one-sided or confirmatory price, without the system ever asking whether the analyst intended to cherry-pick. Whether a comparison is paired or clustered is computed statically from the artifact schema, from declared functional dependencies between key fields alone and before any data is read, and a mechanism that assumes that structure away is refused rather than priced. Because the wealth transformer is antitone in the realized p-value, affordability can be checked before the analysis runs too, turning pre-registration into a typing rule. We prove the metatheory machine-checked in Lean 4 with no admitted gaps, and demonstrate the approach on a reference implementation and two case studies on published artifacts, the SWE-bench Verified leaderboard and BIG-Bench Hard.

### [4] [Information Flow Control in Off-Chain Components](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26858)
*Stian Lybech,Eun-Young Kang,Riccardo Tonello,Anders Dalskov*

Main category: cs.CR

TL;DR: 本文针对具有链下组件的区块链架构，开发了一种智能合约语言的模型。链下组件在区块链节点网络之外的指定位置执行，但与链上合约状态保持同步。文章利用静态信息流控制技术研究链上与链下组件之间数据的完整性和保密性问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 链下组件为开发者提供了更大的灵活性（如接入外部数据、跨链桥接），但也可能引入新的安全漏洞。作者希望形式化建模这类架构，并研究其安全属性，特别是数据完整性与保密性。

Method: 本文构建了一个智能合约语言的形式化模型来描述链上-链下架构，并采用静态信息流控制技术来分析数据在链上与链下组件之间的流动安全问题。模型中链下组件被视为独立线程。

Result: 研究发现，即使在缺乏循环构造的情况下，静态信息流控制也无法确保数据的安全性和保密性。原因是链下组件作为独立线程运行，可以通过递归方法调用编码阻塞构造，从而绕过信息流控制。

Conclusion: 文章结尾讨论了可能的补救方案，以应对链下组件带来的安全挑战。

Abstract: This paper develops a model of a smart-contract language for a blockchain architecture with off-chain components. Off-chain components are pieces of smart contracts that execute at designated locations outside of the network of blockchain nodes, but remain synchronised with the on-chain contract state. They react to changes to the on-chain state, but may also notify the on-chain component about events in the world, e.g. stock prices, weather data etc., or even act as a bridge between different blockchains. This affords greater flexibility for the developer, but may also enable new vulnerabilities. As a concrete example, we use the model to study the problem of ensuring integrity and secrecy of data between the on-chain and off-chain components, using static information flow control techniques. This fails, even in the absence of a loop construct, because off-chain components act as separate threads and can encode a blocking construct e.g. through recursive method calls. We end the paper with a discussion of possible ways to remedy this situation.

### [5] [Spec2Vision: Contract-Guided Delivery of AI-Generated Computer Vision Pipelines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26400)
*Ghfran Jabour,Sergey Ivanov*

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: Spec2Vision是一个实验框架，通过分阶段运行时（合成、筛选、测试、有界修复）在整个流程中保持任务契约的显式性，以生成和评估符合规范约束的计算机视觉代码管线。在17个CV任务、10个可执行条件、850次主运行中达到81/85的评估器测试通过率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 生成的计算机视觉代码即使可运行，也可能不满足下游评估器强制执行的任务契约。现有方法忽视了"可运行性"与"交付满足契约"之间的差距，需要一种显式保持任务契约的框架来弥合这一鸿沟。

Method: 提出Spec2Vision框架，通过分阶段运行时（staged runtime）在各阶段（合成、筛选、测试、有界修复）显式保持任务契约。基准测试覆盖17个CV任务×10个可执行条件×5次重复，共850次主运行，并通过消融实验（移除结构修复、兼容性脚手架、生成器预检）以及多种暴露不同规格信息的单智能体基线进行对比。

Result: Spec2Vision在850次主运行中达到81/85评估器测试通过；移除结构修复降至55/85，移除兼容性脚手架降至58/85，移除生成器预检降至39/85。最强单智能体基线（直接源码规格暴露）仅达35/85；轻量基线虽在85/85中可运行，但仅17/85通过评估器测试、6/85严格交付成功。

Conclusion: 可运行性不等于任务交付。最强证据来自在分阶段的生成、检查与修复过程中显式保持任务契约。该框架证明了在CV代码生成中，将规格契约显式贯穿整个流水线是产生合规交付的关键。

Abstract: Generated computer-vision code can be runnable without satisfying the task contract enforced by a downstream evaluator. We study that gap with Spec2Vision, an experimental framework for producing and evaluating specification-grounded CV pipeline bundles through a staged runtime that keeps the task contract explicit across synthesis, screening, testing, and bounded repair. The benchmark evaluates 17 CV tasks, 10 executable conditions, and 5 repeats per task-condition cell, for 850 primary runs. In the primary 850-run evaluation, Spec2Vision reaches 81/85 evaluator-test passes; removing structural repair drops to 55/85, compatibility scaffolding to 58/85, and generator preflight to 39/85. The executable single-agent baselines expose progressively richer task specifications to the model, culminating in direct source-spec exposure, yet remain much weaker overall, from 17/85 for lightweight task grounding to 35/85 evaluator-test passes. The lightweight baseline nevertheless remains core-runnable in 85/85 runs but reaches only 17/85 evaluator-test passes and 6/85 strict-delivery successes, showing that runnability is not equivalent to delivery. Across this benchmark, the strongest evidence comes from keeping the task contract explicit across staged generation, checking, and repair. Artifacts are provided to support audit of run bundles, model-visible inputs, and derived tables.

</details>
