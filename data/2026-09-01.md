### [1] [Fine-grained Causal Reversibility for Asynchronous Channel-based Programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29225)
*Shunya Oguchi,Shoji Yuen,Nobuko Yoshida,Claudio Antares Mezzina*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出revGo，一个基于Go语言核心的因果可逆调试框架，通过允许重新排序队列中因果无关的消息来实现高效的回滚与重放，突破了传统队列顺序对因果无关操作的约束。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 因果可逆性已被证明是调试并发系统的有效技术，但在基于通道的通信中，队列排序产生的依赖关系阻碍了因果无关操作的回滚与重放，需要分析队列中消息的因果关系并重排无关消息以实现高效的调试。

Method: 提出revGo——假设无界异步通道的Go语言核心。设计了回滚-重放语义，允许在前向执行中无因果关系的消息在队列中被重新排序；通过为通信分配唯一键来实现最小化的回滚与重放。

Result: 证明了重排无关消息不会产生包含不可达进程的配置；通过重排独立消息，利用唯一键标记通信即可实现最小化的回滚与重放。

Abstract: Causal reversibility has emerged as an effective technique for debugging concurrent systems. In particular, rolling back and replaying a concurrent program with causal consistency has been found very helpful in debugging concurrency bugs. In channel-based communication, queue ordering creates dependencies that prevent causally independent actions from being rolled back and replayed. To enable efficient rollback and replay without being constrained by queue dependencies, it is necessary to analyse causal dependencies between messages in queues and reorder independent messages. This paper presents revGo, a core of the Go programming language assuming unbounded asynchronous channels. Our rollback-and-replay semantics allows us to reorder messages in the queue if they are not causally related in the forward execution. It is shown that reordering independent messages generates no configuration with non-reachable processes. By reordering independent messages, rollback and replay are implementable with minimality by assigning unique keys to communications.
