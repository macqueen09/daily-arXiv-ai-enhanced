### [1] [Behavioral Analysis of Timed Actors using Syntactic Slice Equivalence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17840)
*Ali Ataollahi,Fatemeh Ghassemi,Eduard Kamburjan,Marjan Sirjani*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 提出针对Timed Rebeca的静态分析方法，通过比较可观察消息集的后向切片判断能否复用tiny twin，避免重新生成的高昂代价。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 源模型演化时，即使相关行为未改变，重新生成tiny twin仍需状态空间探索与归约，成本极高。

Method: 对Timed Rebeca中给定可观察消息名的Rebeca依赖图进行后向切片比较；针对无Zeno、带after标注且无delay语句的片段，证明切片等价蕴含所选观察下的弱时间双模拟。

Result: 该等价保持可观察动作与内部转换间的总耗时间，从而可复用已有tiny twin；在10个基准模型及保持可观察切片的修订版上实验，静态比较不到1秒、占用数十MB内存，而tiny-twin生成可能超过1小时、占用数百GB。

Conclusion: 静态切片比较成本依图规模，远低于tiny-twin生成的可达状态依赖成本，验证了复用tiny twin的可行性与高效性。

Abstract: Tiny twins are compact behavioral models derived from timed actor models for selected observable messages. When a source model evolves, regenerating its tiny twin requires state-space exploration and reduction even if the relevant behavior is unchanged. We present a static analysis for Timed Rebeca that compares backward slices of Rebeca dependence graphs for a given set of observable message names. For the Zeno-free fragment with after annotations and no delay statements, we prove that slice equivalence implies weak timed bisimulation under the selected observations. This preserves observable actions and total elapsed time across internal transitions, allowing the existing tiny twin to be reused. We evaluate the implementation on ten benchmark models paired with revisions that preserve their observable slices. The cost of static comparison depends on the size of the graphs representing source statements and their dependencies, while tiny-twin generation depends on the number of reachable states and transitions. This difference is reflected in the measurements: static comparison takes less than one second using tens of megabytes of memory, while tiny-twin generation can take over an hour and use hundreds of gigabytes.

### [2] [MSLL: A Runtime Multi-Stack Parsing Approach for Interactive Grammar Development - A Lightweight Extension of LL-Style Recursive Descent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19063)
*Qunhui Zhang*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: MSLL是一种面向编辑期语法探索的递归下降LL解析轻量级运行时扩展：遇FIRST/FIRST冲突时保留多解析栈并跟踪候选产生式，随输入矛盾剪枝，将歧义视为运行时状态管理而非执行前须消除的条件。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 语法原型开发中，作者常需在规则未定或存在重叠备选时先行运行验证。预测式解析器适合稳定语法，但其生成与编译循环拖慢交互式开发效率。因此需要一个能在编辑期直接运行演化中语法、容忍歧义的方案。

Method: MSLL作为递归下降LL解析的运行时扩展，在出现FIRST/FIRST冲突时维持多个活跃解析栈，每个栈跟随不同候选产生式，一旦输入与其矛盾即剪除该栈。针对ANTLR风格工作流，支持直接运行与检查演化中的语法、收集冲突踪迹，并在语法稳定后再翻译或适配到生成式解析器。

Result: DSL类负载上三个1k token基准分别耗时48、49、59 ms；两个歧义50k token负载均低于1秒；500k token深度嵌套压力用例耗时5.395 s。真实JSON语法案例验证了中小输入的交互式表现，同时在500k token处暴露出原型级超线性开销。

Conclusion: MSLL定位于现有LL解析器生成器的开发期辅助工具，而非生产环境解析器的替代品。

Abstract: Few grammars are designed in a straight line. During grammar prototyping, authors often want to run a partially settled rule before refactoring overlapping alternatives or regenerating parser code. Predictive parsers are effective for stable grammars, but their generation and compilation loop can slow down interactive grammar development. This paper presents MSLL, a lightweight runtime extension of recursive-descent LL parsing for edit-time grammar exploration. When a FIRST/FIRST conflict appears, MSLL keeps multiple live parsing stacks, lets each stack follow a different candidate production, and prunes a stack as soon as the input contradicts it. Ambiguity is therefore handled as a runtime state-management problem rather than as a condition that must be removed before execution. The prototype targets an ANTLR-style grammar workflow: run and inspect an evolving grammar directly, collect conflict traces, and later translate or adapt the stabilized grammar for a generated parser. Experiments on DSL-style workloads show that MSLL finishes three 1k-token benchmarks in 48, 49, and 59 ms; stays under one second on two ambiguous 50k-token workloads; and reaches 5.395 s on a 500k-token deeply nested stress case. A real-world JSON grammar case confirms interactive behavior on small and medium inputs while exposing prototype-level super-linear overhead at 500k tokens. The results position MSLL as a development-time companion to existing LL-based parser generators rather than a replacement for production parsers.

</details>

### [3] [Automated Instruction Encoding Synthesis for Modern GPU ISA Compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18662)
*Mingyuan Ma,Hu He*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 提出指令编码合成框架，将指令布局视为约束槽分配问题，用于NVIDIA SASS，可变长编码减少33%指令占用，定长编码减少16%解码器面积。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代GPU内核日益加重指令供给路径压力，而固定指令容器常留有大量空间浪费，需要一种自动化编码合成方法来优化指令布局。

Method: 将指令布局建模为基于验证指令格式字段规格的约束槽分配问题，分离语义字段标识与物理位位置，支持绑定/固定/自由放置约束；对NVIDIA SASS实例化：规范化公开编码文本为机器可读规格，用nvdisasm在3.78M条指令上验证反汇编器并发布开放基准，使用CP-SAT进行定长与变长编码合成。

Result: 在142个Blackwell内核输入上，变长合成减少33%指令占用，Ampere与Hopper重合成结果相当；同规格定长合成相比基于NVIDIA观测128位布局的解码器面积减少16%；生成取指/解码RTL在TSMC 22nm达1.5GHz，面积增量为GA100级芯片的0.12%；SRAM对比显示占用减少约等于该新增逻辑的9倍指令SRAM位单元面积。

Conclusion: 该框架可自动化优化指令编码，在保持性能的同时显著降低指令占用与解码器面积，收益远超所增逻辑开销，具有实用价值。

Abstract: Modern GPU kernels increasingly stress the instruction supply path, while fixed instruction containers can leave substantial footprint slack. This paper presents an automated encoding-synthesis framework that treats instruction layout as a constrained slot-assignment problem over a validated instruction-form field specification. The formulation separates semantic field identity from physical bit positions and supports tied, pinned, and free placement constraints, making it applicable when recurring decoded fields are not frozen by a public format contract. We instantiate the framework for NVIDIA SASS: raw public encoding text is normalized into a machine-readable specification, a SASS disassembler is validated against nvdisasm on 3.78M instructions, released as an open benchmark, and CP-SAT synthesis is used for fixed-length and variable-length encodings. On 142 Blackwell kernel inputs, variable-length synthesis reduces instruction footprint by 33%, with comparable reductions after re-synthesis on Ampere and Hopper; fixed-length synthesis on the same specification reduces decoder area by 16% against a decoder generated from the NVIDIA-observed 128-bit layout by the same generator and flow. Generated fetch/decode RTL meets 1.5 GHz in TSMC 22 nm with a replicated area delta of 0.12% of a GA100-class die; a same-node SRAM comparison shows the footprint reduction corresponds to about 9x this added logic in instruction-SRAM bit-cell area.

</details>
