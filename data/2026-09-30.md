### [1] [Irene: Equivalence Checking of Hybrid Quantum Programs via Structure-Preserving Symbolic Reduction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36065)
*Jingyu Ke,Jingyang Li,Guoqiang Li*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 提出Irene，一个基于结构保持符号归约的混合量子程序等价性检查框架，在1982对程序上达79.92%覆盖率，并发现15个编译器新bug。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 验证混合量子程序编译变换需要等价性检查，但测量依赖控制限制了酉推理，且经典结果与量子操作的依赖会放大中间符号状态。

Method: 基于结构保持符号归约，分三层推理：门级用代数恒等式简化酉区域；混合路径和(HPS)级用类型图表示符号执行状态并以图同构证明等价；剩余义务用密度核刻画输入密度算符到可观测输出的变换。残余系数差异编码为SMT查询；共同符号归约保持因式化布尔与算术表达式。

Result: 在7个基准套件的1982对程序上，Irene解决1584对(79.92%)，覆盖最高基线MQT QCEC为57.52%，平均端到端每对3.93秒；作为等价性检查预言机发现Qiskit、Cirq、PennyLane等15个未知bug。

Conclusion: Irene通过多层次结构保持归约有效处理混合量子程序等价性，显著优于现有检查器，并具备实用bug发现能力。

Abstract: Equivalence checking is essential for validating compiler transformations of hybrid quantum programs, which combine quantum operations, measurements, and classical control. Measurement-dependent control limits unitary reasoning, while dependencies between classical outcomes and quantum operations can enlarge intermediate symbolic states. We present Irene, an equivalence-checking framework for bounded hybrid quantum programs based on structure-preserving symbolic reduction. The framework progressively simplifies equivalence obligations through three levels of reasoning. At the gate level, algebraic identities simplify unitary regions. At the hybrid path-sum (HPS) level, reduced symbolic execution states are represented as typed graphs, whose isomorphism certifies equivalence. Remaining obligations are handled by density kernels that characterize transformations of input density operators into observable outputs, allowing comparison even when internal measurement histories differ. Residual coefficient differences are encoded as SMT queries. A common set of symbolic reductions supports HPS and density-kernel reasoning by preserving factored Boolean and arithmetic expressions, eliminating reducible dependencies before expanding residual sums. We evaluate Irene against five equivalence checkers on 1,982 program pairs from seven benchmark suites. Irene solves 1,584 pairs (79.92%), compared with 57.52% for MQT QCEC, the baseline with the highest aggregate coverage, with a mean end-to-end time of 3.93 seconds per solved pair. Applied as an equivalence-checking oracle, Irene also identifies 15 previously unknown bugs in quantum compilers, including Qiskit, Cirq, and PennyLane.

### [2] [DatalogBench: Evaluating Large Language Models on Text-to-Datalog Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37233)
*Yuan Li,Hanyun Jiang,Guowei Tian,Chengpeng Wang,Peisen Yao*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 该论文提出 DatalogBench，一个包含 136 个 text-to-Datalog 合成任务的基准，用执行结果（而非文本匹配）评分，系统评估了 6 个 LLM 与 4 种提示配置及两类编码智能体。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Datalog 广泛用于程序分析等推理任务，但手工编写困难；现有合成器需用户提供输入-输出示例。LLM 提供了从自然语言直接合成 Datalog 的更自然路径，但其效果此前缺乏系统评估。

Method: 从已有 Datalog 工件中整理 136 个 text-to-Datalog 任务构建 DatalogBench；用留出输入上的执行结果与经变异分析验证的 oracle 对比来评分；评估 6 个 LLM、4 种提示配置和 2 个编码智能体。

Result: 精确匹配最高 68.4%；关系描述或输入-输出示例仅有有限且依赖模型的影响。直接提示下多数失败发生在编译期，典型原因是模型构造了未声明或类型不一致的辅助谓词。两个编码智能体最高达 83.8%，基本消除此类失败，剩余多为集中于递归任务的语义错误。

Conclusion: DatalogBench 指出递归推理与分解是当前 LLM 和智能体的开放挑战，并提供了可靠的、以执行为依据的评测手段。

Abstract: Datalog underpins reasoning tasks such as program analysis, but its programs are hard to write. Existing synthesizers automate this task but require users to state their intent as input-output examples. Large language models (LLMs) suggest a more natural route, text-to-Datalog synthesis from a natural-language question, yet how well they do so has not been systematically evaluated. We present DatalogBench, a benchmark of 136 text-to-Datalog synthesis tasks curated from existing Datalog-based artifacts. Synthesized programs are graded by execution on held-out inputs against an oracle validated by mutation analysis. Across six LLMs and four prompting configurations, exact match peaks at 68.4%, and relation descriptions or an input-output example have only modest, model-dependent effects. Under direct prompting, most failures occur at compile time, typically because a model invents auxiliary predicates that it never declares or types consistently. Two coding agents reach up to 83.8% and eliminate nearly all such failures, leaving mostly semantic errors concentrated in recursive tasks. DatalogBench thus identifies recursive reasoning and decomposition as open challenges for current LLMs and agents, and offers a reliable, execution-grounded measure of both.

### [3] [Modelling Shared-Space Coordination in mCRL2: a Bach-to-mCRL2 Translation Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37726)
*Corentin Reuther,Jean-Marie Jacquet*

Main category: cs.LO

TL;DR: 该论文提出将协调语言Bach自动翻译为mCRL2，显式建模共享空间，从而借助mu-演算模型检测器验证超越可达性的复杂时序性质（如活性、不变式），并提供共享空间数据可达性的系统分析方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 基于数据的协调语言在可靠性验证上研究不足；现有工具如Anemone仅支持Bach程序的可达性验证，无法表达活性性质或共享空间内容的不变式（如"请求总被响应""消息不被静默丢失"），限制了系统行为的全面刻画。

Method: 提出从Bach到mCRL2的自动翻译，显式表示共享空间，利用mCRL2的mu-演算模型检测器进行验证；并引入一套系统方法，通过在mu-演算中直接表达共享空间内容性质来分析其数据可达性。

Result: 实现了超越简单可达性的复杂性质验证，形成一种结合基于动作性质与基于共享空间内容状态性质的新型验证流程，为协调语言验证提供了新的分析维度。

Conclusion: 该方法弥补了现有协调语言自动化验证在表达力上的不足，开辟了动作性质与共享空间状态性质联合验证的新方向，是当前协调语言验证研究中较少涉及的新领域。

Existing tools, such as Anemone, provide a solid foundation for reachability-based verification of Bach programs. While they effectively analyze properties expressed in terms of state attainability, extending support to more expressive temporal specifications-such as liveness properties or invariants over shared space contents-remains an open opportunity. Such extensions are important to capture comprehensive system behaviors, for instance, ensuring that "a request is always matched by a response" or that "no message is silently lost". Addressing this limitation, we propose an automated translation from Bach to mCRL2, which explicitly represents the shared space, thereby enabling the use of mCRL2's mu-calculus model checker to verify complex properties beyond simple reachability. Complementing this translation, we introduce a systematic method to analyze the shared space in mCRL2 concerning data reachability, by directly expressing properties over the shared space's contents in the mu-calculus, thus providing a clearer framework for verification. This approach enables a novel verification process that combines action-based properties with state-based properties over the shared space contents, a largely unexplored area in current coordination-language verification approaches, offering a new dimension of analysis

### [4] [Formal Reasoning about Performance Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37728)
*Moussa Labbadi,Rupak Majumdar,V. R. Sathiyanarayana,Sadegh Soudjani*

Main category: cs.LO

TL;DR: 本文提出一个核心命令式演算，统一刻画离散事件仿真的关键构件，并在其上建立关于几乎必然可达性和期望到达时间的可靠且完备的证明规则，还在Lean中实现工具并完成多个案例验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 离散事件仿真工具广泛用于系统性能分析，但其模型正确性与性能保证的推理基本停留在统计解读层面，缺乏演绎推理的逻辑基础。

Method: 提出核心命令式演算，涵盖异步执行、对分布的连续/离散采样、经全局事件队列的基于时间的事件调度；在其上构建证明系统以推理几乎必然可达性与期望到达时间，并给出可靠且完备的证明规则。

Result: 证明规则具有可靠性与完备性；在Lean中实现该证明规则，并在客户-服务器（超出排队论解析解）和网络路由协议收敛等案例中成功推导相关性质。

Conclusion: 该框架把离散时间概率程序的演绎推理推广到以连续时间和连续概率分布为核心的性能模型；因操作语义的根本不连续性与测度论难点，其可靠完备性证明比离散时间情形复杂得多。

Abstract: Discrete-event simulation is a standard technique for modelling and analysing the performance of computer systems, networks, and services. Although simulation tools are widely used, reasoning about the correctness and performance guarantees of the models they implement remains largely ad hoc: simulation outputs are interpreted statistically, but there is no logical foundation for deductive reasoning about their behaviour. We present a core imperative calculus that captures the essential constructs common to discrete-event simulators: asynchronous execution, continuous and discrete sampling from distributions, and time-based event scheduling through a global event queue. On top of this calculus, we develop a proof system for reasoning about almost-sure reachability and expected reaching time properties. Our main result is a sound and complete proof rule for these properties. Our framework generalizes deductive reasoning for discrete-time probabilistic programs to the setting of performance models, in which continuous time and continuous probability distributions are central. We have implemented the proof rules in a tool embedded in Lean. We demonstrate the applicability of our proof rule by deriving proofs of almost-sure reachability and expected reaching time for a number of case studies, including client-server examples that go beyond analytic solutions from queueing theory as well as convergence behaviours in network routing protocols. Establishing the soundness and completeness of our proof rules requires significantly more complex arguments than in the discrete-time setting. This is due to the fundamentally discontinuous nature of the operational semantics and the measure-theoretic challenges of continuous time and probability distributions.
