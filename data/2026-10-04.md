### [1] [Fixing the Fixpoint: A Formal Theory of Convergence Detection for Incremental Recursive Computation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00530)
*Chengxi Yang,Tej Chajed,Thomas Reps*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 论文指出主流增量计算理论DBSP常用的FirstZero不动点检测策略不可靠，精确FPD对一般电路不可能；作者基于DBSP建立收敛检测理论，定义IntConv与StFP检测器，证明其在Datalog等大量电路上既sound又complete，并用Lean形式化验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: DBSP等增量计算理论虽能高效增量递归计算，但依赖运行时不动点检测(FPD)判断迭代是否终止；常用的FirstZero策略在自然情形下也不sound，且对任意含表达性原语的DBSP电路精确FPD不可能，暴露了理论数学规范与实现之间的根本鸿沟。

Method: 以DBSP为核心演算，发展收敛检测的形式化理论：定义内部收敛IntConv（对应实现中常用的内部状态稳定策略）作为声明式判据；定义状态不动点StFP谓词，构造sound且complete的StFP检测器；将其与固定输入、零输出检查结合，得到sound且complete的IntConv检测器。

Result: 证明IntConv是外部收敛的充分条件；对包括Datalog查询、嵌套while查询及其增量优化版本在内的一大类电路，证明IntConv不仅sound而且complete；所有结果在Lean中形式化验证，代码已开源。

Conclusion: 该理论填补了增量计算中收敛检测从数学规范到实现的鸿沟，为大量实用电路上的收敛检测提供语义保证。

Abstract: Modern incremental computation theories like DBSP have enabled efficient incrementalization of general recursive computations. To do so, they require a runtime Fixpoint Detection (FPD) mechanism to detect whether an iterative computation has reached the fixpoint and thus should terminate. However, we show that the commonly suggested "FirstZero" strategy is unsound even in naturally arising cases, and that exact FPD is impossible for arbitrary DBSP circuits with expressive primitive nodes. This issue reveals a fundamental gap between the mathematical specification and implementations of such theories. To fill this gap, using DBSP as a core calculus, we develop a formal theory of convergence detection. Within this theory, we define internal convergence (IntConv) as a declarative criterion corresponding to the internal-state-stability strategy used by practical implementations, and prove that IntConv is a sufficient condition for external convergence. We then define the state fixpoint (StFP) predicate and a sound and complete StFP detector. Combining the StFP detector with fixed-input and zero-output checks yields a sound and complete IntConv detector. Moreover, for a large class of useful circuits (programs) including Datalog queries, nested while queries, and their incrementally optimized versions, we show that IntConv is not only sound but also complete (meaning any convergence in the theory implies the convergence in our criterion). As a result, our theory provides semantic guarantees for convergence detection on all these circuits. Our results are formally verified in Lean, with the formalization available at https://github.com/Arcadia-Y/fixing-the-fixpoint/.

</details>


### [2] [Associativity and Commutativity in Equality Saturation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01198)
*Tarik Rosin,Marcel Ullrich,Sebastian Hack*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出一种扩展关系型e-matching的方法，将项存储为多重集，以原生支持结合-交换（AC）算子，从而缓解e-graph指数膨胀问题，初步结果表明在特定情况下可渐进地减少内存使用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有e-graph实现在即使简单的例子中也会指数级增长，而许多实际应用涉及AC（结合-交换）算子，亟需原生支持以减少膨胀。

Method: 扩展关系型e-matching，通过将项表示为多重集（multiset）来原生处理AC算子，实现equality saturation modulo AC。

Result: 初步结果显示，在特定情况下，模AC的equality saturation使用的内存渐进地（asymptotically）更少。

Conclusion: 原生支持AC算子可有效缓解e-graph指数膨胀，是提升equality saturation实用性的有前景方向。

Abstract: Equality saturation is a promising technique for program optimization which sidesteps the phase ordering problem. However, current e-graph implementations grow exponentially large, even for simple examples. Many practical applications involve associative and commutative (AC) operators. We present an extension of relational e-matching that handles AC operators natively by storing terms as multisets. Preliminary results show that equality saturation modulo AC uses asymptotically less memory in certain cases.

</details>


### [3] [Augmenting Rewrite Rule Sets via Knuth-Bendix Completion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01246)
*Michael Schifferer,Marcel Ullrich,Sebastian Hack*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文评估用Knuth-Bendix Completion (KBC)自动生成和扩充EqSat的重写规则，实验表明优化更快，并能发现此前遗漏的优化、达到更优结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Equality Saturation依赖重写规则来系统探索候选程序空间以克服阶段排序问题，但其可行性和性能高度依赖具体规则集。这些规则集通常手工构造，需要大量领域知识，且可能遗漏有价值的变换。

Method: 将Knuth-Bendix Completion (KBC)作为自动生成和扩充EqSat重写规则的方法进行评测。

Result: 实验显示优化速度更快，并能达到更优的项，发现了此前遗漏的优化。

Conclusion: KBC可作为自动生成/扩充EqSat重写规则的有效手段，缓解手工规则集的依赖与遗漏问题，提升EqSat优化的效率与效果。

Abstract: Equality Saturation (EqSat) is a powerful technique for program optimization, systematically exploring the search space of candidate programs to overcome the phase ordering problem.
  However, the feasibility and performance of EqSat rely heavily on the specific rewrite rules used to derive equivalent programs. These rule sets are typically handcrafted, requiring extensive domain expertise and carrying the risk of missing valuable transformations.
  In this work, we evaluate Knuth-Bendix Completion (KBC) as a method for automatically generating and augmenting rewrite rules for EqSat. Our experiments show faster optimization as well as reaching better terms with previously missed optimizations.

</details>
