### [1] [Coco: An Agentic Copilot for the Hardware--Software Co-Design Lifecycle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02376)
*Samuel Kushnir,Kavya Sreedhar,Yeshwanth Reddy Pogula,Amir Yazdanbakhsh,Narges Shahidi,Ming Liu,Varun Gohil,Ravi Iyer,Parthasarathy Ranganathan,Christina Delimitrou,Suvinay Subramanian*

TL;DR: Coco 是一个部署于 TPU 架构师团队的智能体平台，通过四层架构（数据存储、工具库、工作流智能体、平台交互）加速 ML 模型与加速器协同设计，避免 LLM 在缺乏外部文献时的幻觉问题。

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: Coco 采用四层设计：(i) 数据存储层自动将每次仿真扫描注册进规范化关系模式，使智能体通过 SQL 查询定位每个数字；(ii) 带有类型化 API 的工具库，供智能体自主编排；(iii) 编码重复分析工作流的智能体，尤其是等执行分析（比较匹配执行配置的系统，包括 Pareto 前沿外被支配的扫描点）；(iv) 平台 UX，其导航状态兼作智能体上下文。

Abstract: Co-designing ML models and the accelerators that run them is an unusual reasoning task: architects must draw confident, high-stakes conclusions about systems that do not yet exist, and the pace of both model evolution and hardware cadence means the analysis burden grows every quarter. We report early deployment experience toward a reduction in time-to-simulation and time-to-insight, and argue that co-design is a distinct agentic domain: its data must be retrieved rather than memorized, its workflows are recurring but context-dependent, and expert adoption hinges on UX that balances IDE-style control with interactive exploration.

### [3] [Bao: Automatic Region Placement and Memory Allocation for Intermittent Computing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02624)

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: Bao 将间歇计算中的区域划分与内存分配联合建模为混合整数线性规划（MILP），在 LLVM 中实现，保证能量安全并显著减少检查点开销。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 提出 Bao 系统，将能量感知的区域形成与内存分配决策进行联合优化，形式化为混合整数线性规划（MILP），以获得全局最优解并提供形式化正确性保证。理论上证明任意可行解均满足能量安全，并在 LLVM 中实现该方案。

Abstract: Intermittent computing enables batteryless embedded devices to operate in harsh environment, but frequent power failures interrupt program execution and require careful management of state across power cycles. The core challenge is to guarantee both memory consistency and forward progress while minimizing the energy overhead of checkpointing. Recent compile-time approaches identify regions that fit in the energy buffer. At runtime, the device waits and recharges between the regions. However, they still rely on greedy or path-local heuristics that commit to local decisions and can miss globally lower-overhead boundary placements.

We prove that any feasible solution guarantees energy safety and implement our approach in LLVM. Our evaluation on 13 benchmarks across 3 capacitor sizes shows that Bao outperforms existing baselines, achieving 10% faster execution and 52% fewer region boundary hits on average compared to the best baseline.

### [5] [OpenMP Meta-Lowering: A Declarative Approach to Performance Portable Parallel Code Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03571)

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 并行硬件日益多样化，现有 OpenMP 编译器将前端语义与固定 lowering 策略紧耦合，限制了跨运行时与架构的性能可移植性。

Method: 基于 MLIR 框架，将 OpenMP 前端与指定 lowering 方式的 DSL 结合，把 lowering 视为可编程组件，从而对代码 outlining、数据共享和运行时接口进行显式控制，并将 OpenMP 构造保留至后期 lowering 阶段。

Result: 在 PolyBench/C-OMP 上针对通用与嵌入式多核目标评测，性能与最先进编译器工具链相当，代码体积开销可忽略（<0.7%）；用 5,182 行代码表达相同 OpenMP 子集，比 Clang 少约 32%，比 GCC 少约 76%；支持 pmsis 运行时仅需数行规范。

Abstract: The increasing diversity of parallel hardware challenges existing compilation flows. While OpenMP provides a portable abstraction for shared-memory parallelism, existing compilers tightly couple the frontend semantics with fixed lowering strategies. This design limits performance portability across different runtimes and architectures. Our approach combines an OpenMP frontend with a domain-specific language (DSL) that specifies how OpenMP constructs are lowered for a target. We call this approach OpenMP meta-lowering, treating lowering as a programmable component rather than compiler-specific logic. This design provides explicit control over code outlining, data sharing, and runtime interfacing across diverse targets. We evaluate our approach on PolyBench/C-OMP across general-purpose and embedded multicore targets. Our results show that the proposed method matches the performance of state-of-the-art compiler toolchains with negligible code-size overhead (< 0.7%). Preserving OpenMP constructs until late lowering stages enables both general-purpose and OpenMP-specific MLIR optimizations. By decoupling lowering from compiler internals, our approach expresses the same OpenMP subset in 5,182 lines of code, ~32% fewer than the corresponding lowering code in Clang and ~76% fewer than in GCC, while supporting the pmsis runtime costs only a few specification lines, enabling rapid support for new runtimes without modifying the compiler.

### [8] [Symbolic Execution of Constrained Horn Clauses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03345)

Main category: cs.LO

TL;DR: 本文提出用约束归结（constrained resolution）作为求解CHC可满足性的演算，证明其与前向/后向符号执行及模型检查算法互补，并扩展至k-归纳与不正确性逻辑。
