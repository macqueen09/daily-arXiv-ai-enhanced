### [1] [Normalization by Evaluation with Substitutions as Operations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21131)
*Jason Z. S. Hu*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文研究无类型域模型下正规化推断（NbE）中把替换定义为操作的设定，而非显式替换；结果显示该设定可简化语法性质与NbE可靠性证明，但使完备性证明变复杂。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 以往NbE工作采用显式替换，其实只是为简化后续语义证明的技术性选择，缺乏检验定义完备性的自然引理，因此作者想考察把替换当作操作的典型设定。

Method: 在无类型域模型下以替换为操作（而非显式替换）的形式化方式重新开展NbE的技术性研究，对比两种设定下的证明结构。

Result: 该设定简化了语法性质的证明以及NbE可靠性（soundness）的证明，但引入了NbE完备性（completeness）证明上的复杂性。

Conclusion: 显式替换并非必需；以替换为操作的设定在证明结构上是一种有价值的取舍，其代价主要集中在完备性证明。

Abstract: In previous works on normalization by evaluation (NbE) with an untyped domain model, explicit substitutions are a major decision choice, which, in retrospect, is taken to simplify subsequent semantic proofs. This choice hence is taken for a purely technical reason. In addition, there is no obvious lemma to test the completeness of the definition or the laws for explicit substitutions. In this technical report, we investigate NbE in a typical setting, i.e. defining substitutions as operations. We show that this setting brings simplifications in proving syntactic properties and soundness of NbE, though complications are introduced in the completeness proof of NbE.

### [2] [Are We Testing the Wrong Quantum Bugs? A Study of Fault Representation Gaps in Quantum Software Testing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22089)
*Asif Mohammed Saad,Ahsanul Ameen Sabit,Jonah Brandenburgh,Jiyuan Wang,Qian Zhang,Soneya Binta Hossain*

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: 该论文审计4个量子变异测试工具和3个精选bug基准，并从10个量子软件仓库收集410个真实bug，构建56个bug家族的统一分类法进行对比。结果显示变异工具仅覆盖20个家族，精选基准补增15个，但只有13.7%的真实bug属于被覆盖家族，86.3%属于两者都未覆盖的家族。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 量子软件依赖编译器、中间表示和执行框架来变换程序，这些组件中的故障会改变原本正确的量子程序行为。然而现有量子测试技术多用变异生成的故障和精选bug基准来评估，作者想探究这些人工产物所代表的故障是否真实反映现代量子软件中实际遇到的故障。

Method: 审计4个量子变异测试工具与3个精选bug基准；从10个量子软件仓库历时两年收集410个经验证的真实bug；将三个来源的故障归类到统一的56个bug家族分类法中，并比较变异算子、精选基准与近期真实bug之间的覆盖情况。

Result: 变异工具支持20个bug家族，精选基准额外增加15个；410个真实bug中仅56个（13.7%）属于两者任一覆盖的家族，其余354个（86.3%）属于两者均缺失的家族。最大的未覆盖家族涉及编译器变换（66个bug）、类型处理（53个）和输入验证（47个）。这些故障往往依赖中间表示、编译器流水线、输入类型或依赖版本，而非局部的门或语句改动。

Conclusion: 现有变异算子主要修改电路和程序构件，而许多真实故障出现在处理这些构件的编译器、表示和框架接口中。要弥合这一差距，需超越电路级故障，转向测试编译器变换中的语义保持、表示边界的正确性以及软件组件间的交互；本研究为构建能捕捉这些机制并保留可复现上下文的故障模型与基准提供了实证依据。

Abstract: Quantum software relies on compilers, intermediate representations, and execution frameworks that transform programs before execution. Faults in these components can change the behavior of otherwise correct quantum programs. Yet quantum testing techniques are often evaluated using mutation-generated faults and curated bug benchmarks. We investigate how well the faults represented by these artifacts reflect those encountered in modern quantum software. We audit four quantum mutation-testing tools and three curated bug benchmarks, and collect 410 verified bugs from ten quantum software repositories over two years. We classify faults from all three sources into a common taxonomy of 56 bug families and compare their representation across mutation operators, curated benchmarks, and recent real-world bugs. The mutation tools support 20 bug families, and curated benchmarks add 15 more. Yet only 56 of the 410 real-world bugs (13.7%) belong to families represented by either source; the remaining 354 (86.3%) belong to families absent from both. The largest unrepresented families concern compiler transformations (66 bugs), type handling (53), and input validation (47). Unlike local gate or statement changes, these faults often depend on intermediate representations, compiler pipelines, input types, or dependency versions. Existing mutation operators primarily modify circuits and program constructs, whereas many real-world faults arise in the compilers, representations, and framework interfaces that process them. Addressing this gap requires looking beyond circuit-level faults toward testing semantic preservation across compiler transformations, correctness at representation boundaries, and interactions between software components. Our study provides empirical evidence to guide fault models and benchmarks that capture these mechanisms and preserve the contexts needed to reproduce them.

### [3] [FAOPs: Normal Forms and an Algebra for AI Agent Workflows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22061)
*Ali Mohammadi Shanghooshabad*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 提出FAOP形式化理论，将AI智能体工作流建模为带类型、作用域状态的函数，通过四级范式（分解/类型化/抽取复用/最小权限）使完整性、隔离、类型、终止性和成本成为编译期保证，并在SkillBase系统中实现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: AOP（智能体操作流程）在受监管行业必须可追踪、可审计、可复现、访问受控且有时间成本边界，但当前多以粗粒度文件保存，存在步骤捆绑、副本漂移、任意步骤可访问任意工具、重构无校验等问题。

Method: FAOP理论：将工作流视为作用域状态上的类型化函数；四级渐进范式FNF-1分解为原子步骤、FNF-2显式并类型检查数据流、FNF-3共享过程单一规范定义、FNF-4最小权限工具作用域；基于可靠等式的代数将重构与并行化转为编译器重写；正交扩展+V终止验证、+O优化、+B时间成本边界；0-3架构级分类器判断是否需要AI。

Result: 归一化后每个原子步骤成为独立实体，具备声明契约，可单独进行类型检查、作用域限制、边界约束、测试、追踪与审计；实现SkillBase管理系统及独立运行时执行器以在运行期强制保证；以金融工作流为例端到端演示。

Conclusion: FAOP把完整性、隔离、类型、终止、成本与优化提升为整个库的编译期关注点而非运行期期望，为智能体工作流带来数据库级纪律。

Abstract: Organizations increasingly encode operational procedures as multi-step agent skills, here called Agent Operating Procedures (AOPs), that must be traceable, auditable, reproducible, access-controlled, and bounded in time and cost, requirements that regulated sectors make mandatory. Yet AOPs are typically kept as coarse-grained files, in which steps bundle actions, copied procedures drift apart, any step may reach any tool, and refactoring goes unchecked. We present Functional Agent Operating Procedures (FAOP), a formal theory that brings database-grade discipline to AI agent workflows. FAOP recasts each workflow as a typed function over scoped state and subjects an entire library to four progressive normal forms: Decomposed (FNF-1) splits a workflow into atomic steps, Typed (FNF-2) makes its data flow explicit and type-checked, Factored (FNF-3) gives each shared procedure a single canonical definition so a change propagates instead of drifting, and Scoped (FNF-4) confines every step to least-privilege tool access. Once normalized, every atomic step is a separate entity with a declared contract and can be type-checked, scope-confined, bounded, tested, traced, and audited on its own. A FAOP algebra of provably sound equalities turns refactoring and parallelization into compiler rewrites; orthogonal extensions add verified termination (+V), optimization (+O), and time and cost bounds (+B); and an architectural-level classifier (0-3) determines whether a workflow needs AI at all. Together, these make integrity, isolation, typing, termination, cost, and optimization compile-time concerns across the whole library, not runtime hopes. We implement the theory in SkillBase, an AOP management system (what PostgreSQL is to data, SkillBase is to AOPs), together with a separate executor that enforces these guarantees at run time. A worked financial workflow illustrates the approach end to end.

</details>
