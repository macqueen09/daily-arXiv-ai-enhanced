### [1] [Record Types Everywhere](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27519)
*Matthew Toohey,Ara Jamalzadeh,Ningning Xie*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 论文提出一种基于标准记录类型的新演算，用以建模一等环境与函数应用范式，形式化了核心演算并证明了类型可靠性、归一性及对简单类型λ演算的模拟，还扩展到可变引用，全部在 Lean 4 中机械化验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 一等环境与函数应用范式的现有建模方式较为复杂，作者希望用标准记录类型这一简单、有原则的设计来统一表达。

Method: 对通用记录类型提出新扩展，构建核心演算并形式化其语义；证明类型可靠性、归一化及对 STLC 的模拟；进一步加入可变引用扩展；在 Lean 4 中机械化全部定义与性质，并实现经形式化验证与操作语义等价的解释器。

Result: 成功获得了一个简洁且有原则的设计，核心演算及可变引用扩展的语义与所有声明的性质均已在 Lean 4 中完成机械化验证，解释器等价性亦被形式化证明。

Conclusion: 标准记录类型的新扩展足以优雅地建模一等环境与函数应用，且该设计具备良好的理论性质与高可信度的机器验证支持。

Abstract: This paper introduces a novel calculus that models first-class environments and function application paradigms using standard record types. By proposing new extensions to general record types, we achieve a design that is remarkably simple and principled. We formalise this design in a core calculus and prove several desirable properties, including type soundness, normalization, and a simulation of the simply typed lambda calculus. Additionally, we extend the design with mutable references. The calculus, its semantics, along with the extension, and all stated properties are fully mechanized in the Lean 4 proof assistant, alongside an interpreter formally verified to be equivalent to the operational semantics.

</details>


### [2] [Execution-Time Validation of Context-Carrying Action Intents in Industrial Cyber-Physical Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27971)
*Luca D'Agati,Giuseppe Tricomi,Fabio Orazio Mirto,Giovanni Merlino*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 论文提出"执行间隙"问题：工业单元中学习策略/操作员/调度器的命令经推理、确认与传输后才到达控制器，期间工厂状态可能变化（如操作员暂停），导致过期命令仍被执行。作者提出AIS契约（生产者声明有效期与假设）与XAIR运行时（提交前重检、原子授权、版本条件命令），在仿真与物理单元（OpenPLC、ARM、RISC-V）上做受控消融，证明只有控制器扫描内检查能闭合间隙。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代工业控制中命令延迟到达控制系统，而工厂状态可能在延迟期被改变（如操作员暂停），此时命令已不再正确却仍被执行。作者要回答：应由哪个组件执行最后一道检查，以及检查应针对哪个状态的哪个版本。

Method: 提出Action Intent Schema (AIS)：生产者声明决策有效期与前提假设；XAIR运行时在释放前重检意图，可在上下文存储中原子提交授权，并以版本条件命令形式释放，控制器仅在自身状态版本未变时执行。在仿真与物理单元、OpenPLC、ARM与RISC-V板卡上进行带声明真值的受控消融，比较副本检查、控制器局部互锁、版本条件命令，以及全局/读取作用域/谓词真值作用域等版本方案。

Result: 副本上的检查总是留下未保护区间（提交后数毫秒至100ms以上写入的暂停可逃逸）；只有软件控制器扫描内的检查（局部互锁或版本条件命令）能闭合该控制器所拥有状态的间隙，且仅版本条件命令在暂停被撤销后仍能拒绝命令。全局版本会因无关更新撤销所有有效命令；按意图读取范围或按谓词真值作用域的版本不会；对读取变量有连续信号时，只有谓词真值作用域避免了误撤销，但随谓词累积加重存储负担。单嵌入式核上XAIR达148–168意图/秒。

Conclusion: 最终检查必须放在拥有该状态的软件控制器扫描内才有效；版本条件命令是唯一同时处理暂停与暂停撤销的方案；版本作用域应限定在意图读取的内容或其谓词真值上，后者避免误撤销但需权衡存储开销。XAIR以中等吞吐量提供了可行实现。

Abstract: In industrial cells, commands from learned policies, operators and schedulers reach the controller after inference, confirmation and transport, and the plant may have changed meanwhile, for instance by an operator pause. Such a command is released, or takes effect, when it is no longer correct. We call this the execution gap and ask which component should make the last check, and against which version of which state. The Action Intent Schema (AIS) is a contract in which a producer states how long its decision stays valid and what it assumes. The runtime XAIR rechecks each intent before release, can commit its authorization atomically in the context store, and can release it as a version-conditional command, which the controller executes only if its version of the state is unchanged. In controlled ablations with declared ground truth on emulated and physical cells, OpenPLC and ARM and RISC-V boards, a check on a replica always left an unprotected interval: a pause written from a few to over 100ms after submission escaped it. Only a check in the scan of a software controller, either a controller-local interlock or the version-conditional command, closed the interval for the state that controller owns; only the version-conditional command also refused commands after a pause that had been undone. A global version revoked up to every valid command under unrelated updates. Versions scoped to what the intent reads, or to the truth of its predicates, did not; with continuous signals on the variables the intent reads, only the latter avoided false revocations, though it burdens the store as predicates accumulate. On one embedded-board core, XAIR validated 148-168 intents/s.

</details>


### [3] [What is a Good Projection?: Correctness and Separation Results of End-Point Projections](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28525)
*Jake Masters,Nobuko Yoshida*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 论文提出"好的投影"通用判据，使类型健全性、无死锁和活性可自动导出，并用其重新审视文献中的投影算法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多重会话类型（MPST）通过全局类型投影到局部类型来保证分布式程序正确性，但其类型健全性很脆弱：每换一个投影算法、语义或正确性目标，都需重新证明。作者希望摆脱这种逐案证明的局面。

Method: 给出一组"好投影"的通用要求（判据），只要投影算法满足这些要求，就能自动推出类型健全性、无死锁与活性。随后把判据扩展到混合选择全局类型和异步通信优化，并提出新的"好投影"算法；再利用好投影的性质推导投影算法与MPST类型系统之间的分离性与表达力结果。最后实现一个对投影算法模块化的类型系统，支持混合选择。

Result: 用判据检验文献中的投影：在同步与异步语义下证明8种不同投影是"好的"，另4种不正确、不满足类型健全性。给出新的适用于混合选择和异步优化的好投影算法，得到分离性与表达力结论，并实现模块化系统。

Conclusion: 好投影判据把"逐案证明"转为"一次性判据"，使类型健全性与活性可自动获得；分离性结果帮助程序员在复杂度与表达力之间做有依据的权衡，而理论保证所选算法被正确应用。

Abstract: \emph{Multiparty session types} (MPST) is a type discipline for ensuring distributed programs are implemented correctly \emph{by construction}. MPST starts by specifying a choreographic protocol as a \emph{global type} and \emph{projects} it onto each participant, generating \emph{local types} with a guarantee of type soundness and deadlock-freedom. A central issue of MPST is that its type soundness is fragile, as it must be proven each time with respect to a chosen projection algorithm under specific semantics with a specific program correctness goal. To overcome this problem, we propose general criteria for a \emph{good projection} -- a set of requirements for projection algorithms by which type soundness, deadlock-freedom and liveness of distributed programs are \emph{automatically} derived. Using the criteria, we prove that 8 distinct projections in the literature are good under both synchronous and asynchronous semantics, and that 4 are incorrect, failing type soundness. We extend the good projection criteria to mixed choice global types and asynchronous communication optimisations, proposing new ``good projection'' algorithms. We apply properties of good projections to prove \emph{separation} and \emph{expressiveness} results over the projection algorithms and MPST typing systems. Having a generalised typing system that is robust with respect to the good projection criteria, we provide an implementation with mixed choice global types that is \emph{modular} with respect to a chosen projection algorithm and desired program properties. Our separation results help programmers make informed trade-offs between complexity and expressiveness when choosing projection algorithms, and our theory ensures their chosen algorithms are correctly applied.

</details>

### [4] [ExeBench-FHD: Addressing Fidelity Issues in Decompiler Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28212)
*Akarsh Ashok,Dinil Mon Divakaran,Vivek Balachandran*

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: 作者分析广泛使用的 LLM 反编译器评测基准 ExeBench，指出 9 个影响评测保真度的问题，并提出向后兼容的修订版 ExeBench-FHD，新增 16 个字段，实验证明可显著提升 GPT 6 Sol 的重执行率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ExeBench 被广泛用于评估基于 LLM 的反编译器，但其测试对"反编译结果是否忠实"的度量存在缺陷：部分问题使 LLM 根本无法从 ExeBench 的函数中恢复 C 代码，另一些问题则影响测试有效性，因此需要识别并修正这些问题。

Method: 论文将问题归纳为两类。第一类（3 个）妨碍代码恢复：整型与浮点混合时的参数顺序歧义、优化汇编可能丢弃的未使用参数、存放在函数汇编之外的常量。第二类（6 个）影响测试有效性：非 void 函数缺少 return、占位类型、被优化器删除的 static/inline 函数、名为 main 的函数、平凡函数体、被截断的定义。基于此提出 ExeBench-FHD：向后兼容修订版，新增 16 个字段，包括调用点汇编、数据段、测试包装器、链接后二进制反汇编及 5 个警告标志。

Result: ExeBench 中 38.9% 的函数至少带有一个警告标志；剔除带有四个警告标志（即除 unused_param 外的全部，unused_param 标记的函数经新包装器可继续评估）的记录后，仍保留语料库的 63.9%。在四种架构、-O2 优化下的评测中，新字段使 GPT 6 Sol 的重执行率分别提升最高 26.5%（读取数据段的函数）、19.1%（混合参数类型的函数）和 21.0%（含未使用参数的函数）。

Conclusion: ExeBench 作为基准存在系统性的保真度与有效性缺陷，会低估 LLM 反编译器的实际能力。ExeBench-FHD 通过补充调用点汇编、数据段、包装器、链接后反汇编与警告标志，使这些函数可被正确评估，显著提升重执行率，且保持向后兼容。

Abstract: ExeBench is one of the most widely used benchmarks for evaluating LLM-based decompilers. We analyze ExeBench and identify nine issues that affect how faithfully a test measures a decompiler. Three of them prevent LLM decompilers from recovering C code from ExeBench's function. These are ambiguous parameter order when integer and floating-point types are mixed, unused parameters that optimized assembly may drop, and constants stored outside the function's assembly code. Six more affect test validity: non-void functions without a return, placeholder types, static or inline functions removed by the optimizer, functions named main, trivial bodies and truncated definitions. We present ExeBench-FHD, a backward-compatible revision of ExeBench that adds 16 new fields: call-site assembly, data sections, test wrappers, linked-binary disassembly and five warning flags. In ExeBench, 38.9% of functions have at least one of the five flags; dropping records with any of the four warning flags (all except unused_param, which marks functions that the new wrappers make evaluable) keeps 63.9% of the corpus. In an evaluation at -O2 on four architectures, the new fields raise GPT 6 Sol's re-execution rate by up to 26.5%, 19.1% and 21.0% on functions that read data sections, mix parameter types and have unused parameters, respectively.

</details>

### [5] [A General Construction of Strict Models in HoTT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28370)
*Niyousha Najmaei,Niels van der Weide*

Main category: cs.LO

TL;DR: 在HoTT中实现Voevodsky的universe model构造，使任何pseudomodel配合足够封闭的universe类型都能化归为strict model，并在Rocq/UniMath中形式化，得到presheaf和assembly模型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: HoTT带有外延性原则，适合研究类型论的元理论，尤其适合发展类型论语义。语义中有两类模型：给出可靠解释的strict model和普遍存在但需"严格化"才能转为strict model的pseudomodel。然而常用严格化方法需假定一些具体模型一般不满足的条件，难以迁移到HoTT中。

Method: 在HoTT中开展Voevodsky的universe model构造：给定一个pseudomodel及一个对足够多类型构造子封闭的universe类型，构造出strict model；并说明当universe对这些构造子封闭时，如何在所得模型中解释各类型构造子。

Result: 该构造推广了文献中已有的strict set模型实例，并将其应用于更多例子，在HoTT中得到类型论的presheaf模型与assembly模型；所有结果已在Rocq的UniMath库中形式化。

Conclusion: Universe model构造为在HoTT中把pseudomodel严格化提供了可行方案，统一并推广了已有严格模型，其形式化验证了方法的可靠性。

Abstract: Homotopy type theory (HoTT) provides a natural setting for studying the metatheory of type theory, as it comes with additional extensionality principles compared to ordinary Martin-Löf type theory. In particular, it is especially suitable for developing semantics of type theory, where one distinguishes two kinds of models: strict models, which give us sound interpretations, and pseudomodels. Pseudomodels are useful as they occur prevalently and can be turned into strict models by constructions called strictifications. However, the commonly used strictifications do not translate well to HoTT, as they require assumptions that are generally not satisfied by concrete examples of models.
  We show how to carry out the universe model construction, due to Voevodsky, in HoTT. Under this construction, every pseudomodel together with a universe type closed under enough type formers gives rise to a strict model. We also show how to interpret type formers in the resulting model assuming that the universe is closed under them. Finally, we demonstrate that this construction generalises existing instances of strict set models in the literature, and we apply it to more examples, yielding presheaf models and assembly models of type theory in HoTT. Our results are formalised in Rocq using the UniMath library.

</details>
