### [1] [Runtime Semantic Discovery for Evolving Action Alphabets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28682)
*Pietro Bernabei,Rosario Pugliese*

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: 本文提出基于执行轨迹中动作邻居上下文的"局部、运行时"动作相似性定义，定义一族 k-邻居等价，用于发现演化系统中重命名/拆分/合并动作之间的语义对应，从而支持运行时监控器与规约的适配。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 运行时验证通常假设固定的可观测动作字母表，但在软件演化（更新、重构、架构变更）中，动作可能被重命名、拆分或合并而高层意图不变，导致原有监控器和规约与运行系统失配。作者希望在不重建全局行为模型的前提下识别新旧动作的语义对应。

Method: 提出一种局部且可在运行时计算的动作相似性概念：以动作在执行轨迹中出现的邻居上下文为依据，定义一族 k-邻居等价（k-neighbor equivalences），用于比较可观测字母表不同的系统。方法刻意保持轻量，目标不是完整刻画行为保持性，而是识别候选语义对应关系。作者还分析了该等价的表达能力与局限。

Result: 论文讨论了邻居等价在表达能力上的边界与局限，并展示了如何利用推断出的动作对应关系，在若干类字母表演化情形下支持运行时监控器与规约的适配。

Conclusion: 基于邻居上下文的轻量动作等价可作为演化系统中语义对应发现的候选手段，虽不能完全保证行为保持，但在特定字母表演化类别下能有效辅助监控与规约的自适应。

Abstract: Runtime Verification typically assumes a fixed alphabet of observable actions. This assumption becomes fragile in evolving software systems, where updates, reconfigurations, or architectural changes may rename, split, or fuse actions while preserving the system's high-level intent. In these cases, previously valid monitors and specifications may become misaligned with the running system.
  In this paper, we study runtime semantic discovery, namely the problem of identifying whether newly observed actions play the same operational role as previously known ones, without reconstructing a global behavioral model. To this end, we propose a local and runtime-computable notion of action similarity based on the neighbor contexts in which actions occur within execution traces. We define a family of k-neighbor equivalences and use them to compare systems whose observable alphabets differ.
  Our approach is intentionally lightweight: it does not aim to fully characterize behavioral preservation, but rather to identify candidate semantic correspondences between actions in evolving systems. We discuss the expressive power and limitations of neighbor-based equivalence and show how inferred correspondences can support the adaptation of runtime monitors and specifications under selected classes of alphabet evolution.

### [2] [RegoMender: A Multi-agent Framework for Automated Rego Policy Repair](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29456)
*Ruijie Zhang,Huaxiao Yin,Li Hu,Fanqi Kong,Haozhe Liang,Shaoyin Cheng,Kun Zhang,Bibo Tu*

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: 论文提出首个Rego策略自动修复基准RegoBench（1159个真实缺陷）和反馈引导的多智能体修复框架RegoMender，实现82.9%语法与77.9%语义成功率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 云原生系统中Policy-as-Code已成基础范式，但Rego策略因严格语法、声明式语义和特定验证要求而难以修复。现有自动程序修复（APR）方法主要面向主流语言，难以适配Rego特有的策略逻辑与修复流程，导致Rego修复仍依赖人工试错。

Method: 构建RegoBench基准，包含1159个真实世界缺陷。提出RegoMender反馈引导多智能体框架，通过共享黑板和原子工具包协作，动态执行打补丁、重写或回滚操作以应对复杂修复场景。

Result: 在RegoBench上语法与基于测试的语义成功率分别达82.9%和77.9%，超越最强基线13.4%。在单步与链式修复任务中均保持有效性（而其他方法在复杂场景下性能下降）。在HCL和SQL上泛化良好，在132个真实CVE漏洞上达到86.4%修复率。

Conclusion: RegoMender有效解决了Rego策略自动修复难题，兼具鲁棒性与跨语言泛化能力，对安全实践具有实用价值。

Abstract: Policy-as-Code (PaC) has emerged as a foundational paradigm in cloud-native systems. However, repairing Rego policies remains challenging due to their strict syntax, declarative semantics, and specific verification requirements. Existing automated program repair (APR) methods are mainly designed for mainstream languages and struggle to accommodate the unique policy logic and repair workflows of Rego. Thus, Rego policy repair remains a manual process driven by trial and error. To address this, we introduce RegoBench, the first automated Rego policy benchmark comprising 1,159 real-world defects, and propose RegoMender, a feedback-guided multi-agent framework. Collaborating via a shared blackboard and an atomic toolkit, RegoMender dynamically executes patching, rewriting, or rolling back, to resolve complex repair scenarios. Evaluated on RegoBench, RegoMender achieves syntactic and test-based semantic success rates of 82.9\% and 77.9\%, respectively, outperforming the strongest baseline by 13.4\%. Unlike other approaches whose performance drops in complex scenarios, RegoMender maintains effectiveness across single-step and chained repair tasks. Furthermore, it generalizes strongly across HCL and SQL and achieves an 86.4\% repair rate on 132 real-world CVE vulnerabilities, proving its robustness and practical utility for security.

### [3] [Deciding Scale Placement in MXFP4 Dot Products with Integer Arithmetic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29445)
*Shreyas Vinayakumar*

Main category: cs.LO

TL;DR: 本文研究 MXFP4 点积归约中"缩放前置 vs 缩放后置"两种编译器重写的等价性合法性，构建整数状态机并在 QF_LIA 中精确判定逐位等价，给出基于最大真子树规模的闭式上界，并实现为 MLIR lowering 原型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: MXFP4 用共享的 2 的幂缩放表示 4 位元素。编译器 lowering 点积归约时，可在归约前应用缩放，或推迟到归约后。实数域下二者等价，但在 binary32 中因上溢/下溢及归约树结构可能产生不同结果，因此需要判定双向重写是否合法。

Method: 构造整数状态机，利用有限的元素字母表与共享 2 的幂缩放，显式建模上溢、NaN 与次正规舍入；证明其在有界缩放码与整数系数下的忠实性，从而把等价性查询精确编码为无量子线性整数算术（QF_LIA）。对 MXFP4 推导给定二元归约树下缩放指数和的闭式充分上界，其依赖最大真子树大小。实现配以独立浮点编码、穷举/分层 binary32 执行与 GPU 反例回放交叉验证，并做 MLIR lowering 原型用条件作静态或运行时守卫。

Result: 在 k∈{2,3,4,8,16,32} 的四种树形与 k=64 的两种树形上，将上界上调一个指数步长后 SMT 即找到反例。与 QF_FP 直接编码对比，匹配查询下 cvc5 数秒内证明整数版本，而浮点证明即便 k=2 也达到一 CPU 小时限制。

Conclusion: 整数状态机+QF_LIA 编码给出可实用判定的逐位等价性方法，闭式上界紧致（加一即失效），并可通过 MLIR 原型在编译期或运行时安全选择两种归约形式。

Abstract: The Open Compute Project's microscaling (MX) family includes MXFP4, which stores 32 four-bit elements with a shared power-of-two scale. When lowering a dot-product reduction, a compiler can apply these scales before the reduction, or defer scaling until afterwards. Although equivalent over real numbers, these formulations can produce different IEEE binary32 results through overflow or underflow, with equivalence also depending on the reduction tree. We study the legality of compiler rewrites in either direction by deriving conditions under which the two formulations are bitwise equivalent.
  To decide rewrite legality, we construct an integer state machine that exploits the finite element alphabet and shared power-of-two scales, modeling overflow, NaN, and subnormal rounding. We prove its faithfulness under bounded scale codes and integer coefficients, yielding an exact encoding of the equivalence query in quantifier-free linear integer arithmetic (QF_LIA). For MXFP4, we derive a closed-form sufficient upper bound on the sum of scale exponents for any fixed binary reduction tree within the supported coefficient range. This bound depends on the size of the largest proper subtree. For four tree shapes at block lengths $k\in\{2,3,4,8,16,32\}$ and two shapes at $k=64$, SMT finds counterexamples when this upper bound is increased by one exponent step. Against the standard direct encoding in quantifier-free floating-point arithmetic (QF_FP), on matched queries cvc5 proves the integer version in seconds but reaches a configured one-CPU-hour limit on the floating-point proof even at $k=2$. The implementation was cross-checked using an independent floating-point encoding, exhaustive and stratified binary32 execution, and GPU replay of counterexamples. An MLIR lowering prototype demonstrates selection between the two formulations using the certified conditions as static or runtime guards.

</details>
