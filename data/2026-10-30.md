### [1] [Well-Typed ComfyUI Cannot Go Wrong: Gradual Refinement Types for Generative-AI Workflow Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37673)
*Pingchuan Ma,Zhantong Xue,Hui Li,Zhaoyu Wang,Yuguang Zhou,Shuai Wang,Xiaoqin Zhang*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 用LLM分析ComfyUI的GitHub问题揭示语义组合失败普遍存在（8% issue、21%运行时失败、32%静默），提出渐进细化类型系统ComfyTy在执行前检查工作流组件兼容性，检测66.4%注入故障且无误报，并显著提升用户定位与修复准确率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 生成式AI工作流由独立开发的模型与算子拼装而成，其正确性依赖模型族、张量几何、控制模态、量化级别之间的关系；在ComfyUI中即使校验器接受每条连接，这些关系仍可能被违反，导致昂贵的运行时崩溃或静默失败，因此需要量化这类语义组合失败的普遍性并提供执行前的检查手段。

Method: 用LLM对ComfyUI仓库全部8,766个issue和1,368个讨论帖进行分类并做跨模型一致性校验；提出ComfyTy渐进细化类型系统，每个节点声明可复用的schema（接受的模型族、尺寸、模态、量化），连接节点时将schema组合为约束并求解以发现不兼容；通过向官方工作流注入故障并逐一执行进行评估，另在765个已知良好工作流与108个由用户报告重建的真实失败上测试，并开展10人用户研究。

Result: 语义组合失败占issue报告的8%、issue追踪中运行时失败的21%，其中32%为静默失败；ComfyTy检出66.4%的已确认注入故障（均被ComfyUI自身校验器放行），在765个已知良好工作流上零误报，检出108个真实失败中的67%；用户研究中故障定位准确率从35.0%提升至87.5%，修复准确率从30.0%提升至62.5%。

Conclusion: 语义组合失败是生成式AI工作流中显著且常被静默掩盖的问题类别，执行阶段难以可靠暴露；ComfyTy通过执行前的类型约束检查提供了实用的检测手段，并显著改善用户的故障定位与修复效率。

Abstract: Generative-AI workflows are programs assembled from independently developed models and operators. Their correctness depends on relationships among model families, tensor geometry, control modalities, and quantization levels. In ComfyUI, these relationships can be violated even when every connection is accepted by its validator, causing expensive runtime crashes or silent failures. To measure how often such semantic composition failures occur, we classify all 8,766 issues and 1,368 discussion threads of ComfyUI's GitHub repository with an LLM, checked for cross-model agreement. They account for 8\% of issue reports and 21\% of issue-tracker runtime failures, and 32\% of them are silent. Because execution cannot reliably reveal those failures, we present ComfyTy, a gradual refinement type system that checks component compatibility before execution as a compositional property of workflow graphs. Each node declares a reusable schema that states which model families, sizes, modalities, and quantizations it accepts and produces. Wiring nodes together combines their schemas into constraints, which ComfyTy solves to find and report incompatibilities. To evaluate ComfyTy, we first inject faults into official ComfyUI workflows and execute every resulting graph. ComfyTy detects 66.4\% of the confirmed injected faults, all of which ComfyUI's own validator admits. On real workflows across more model families, it raises no false alarm on 765 known-good workflows and detects 67\% of 108 failures rebuilt from user reports. Finally, in a user study with 10 participants, its diagnostics raise fault-localization accuracy on faulty workflows from 35.0\% to 87.5\% and repair accuracy from 30.0\% to 62.5\%.

### [2] [Automated Verification of Hamiltonian Simulation by Quantum Signal Processing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39491)
*Min Lai,Yu-Fang Chen,Shih-Han Hung*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 首个全自动验证框架，用于验证基于QSP的哈密顿量模拟电路，利用算法的结构化抽象实现可扩展的形式化验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 量子哈密顿量模拟电路规模巨大（宏展开后约152万门），直接形式化验证不可行，需要利用算法本身的结构（Pauli和、块编码、QSP）来做抽象化验证。

Method: 提出面向ancilla索引电路块的符号化Pauli和语义；证明全零ancilla块与目标哈密顿量成比例并自动推断次归一化因子；再通过提取QSP层实现的多项式变换并证明其逼近误差来验证QSP层；块编码只需验证一次，QSP层通过抽象接口检查而无需展开oracle调用。

Result: 可验证系统量子比特数达256的结构化Pauli和哈密顿量块编码；可验证QSP哈密顿量模拟实例，提取实现的多项式并检查逼近界；组合验证的块编码与QSP封装，宏展开规模约152万门。

Conclusion: 利用算法的结构化抽象，全自动验证能够处理大规模常见哈密顿量模拟电路。

Abstract: We present, to the best of our knowledge, the first fully automated verification framework for QSP-based Hamiltonian simulation. Our framework exploits the structure of the simulation algorithm: Hamiltonians are given as weighted sums of Pauli strings, embedded into larger unitaries through block encodings, and transformed into time-evolution operators by quantum signal processing (QSP). Using a symbolic Pauli-sum semantics for ancilla-indexed circuit blocks, our verifier proves that the distinguished all-zero ancilla block is proportional to the target Hamiltonian and infers the subnormalization factor automatically. It then verifies the QSP layer by extracting the implemented polynomial transformation and certifying its approximation error. This structure-aware abstraction is essential for scalability. Composing our verified block encoding and QSP wrapper yields an estimated macro-expanded size of about 1.52 million gates. The verifier certifies the block encoding once and checks the QSP layer through its abstract interface, without expanding the oracle calls. Our implementation verifies block encodings of structured Pauli-sum Hamiltonians with up to 256 system qubits and verifies QSP Hamiltonian-simulation instances by extracting their implemented polynomials and checking the resulting approximation bounds. These results show that fully automated verification can handle common Hamiltonian-simulation circuits at substantial scale when it exploits their algorithmic structure.

### [3] [Countable Nondeterminism in Weighted Programming with an Application to Mechanized Foundations for Probabilistic Program Verification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39723)
*Sebastian Körner,Oliver Emil Bøving,Christoph Matheja*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文将加权编程从半环扩展到模，引入可数非确定性与用户自定义可加奖励，在WDP操作语义下给出了wp推理可靠性的条件，并证明多种实例可靠，且在Lean中完成机械化，得到含可数非确定的MDP最小期望奖励不动点刻画的首个机械化证明。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 原始加权编程只能刻画期望结果与在线算法竞争分析，缺乏对可数非确定性和资源消耗/博弈最优得分的支持；而直接在任意模上套用wp演算会因可数非确定下连续性失效而不可靠，因此需要明确可靠性条件。

Method: 将加权程序参数化于模而非半环，加入可数非确定与用户定义可加奖励；以加权决策过程（WDP，推广MDP，转移权重与状态奖励取自模）定义操作语义真值；研究wp式变换器相对该真值可靠的充分条件，并据此构造若干具体实例。

Result: 给出wp变换器可靠的若干条件，证明加权编程框架的一系列具体实例（含概率程序期望资源消耗与组合博弈最优得分）享有可靠的wp推理；通过不同模的案例研究展示其实用性，并在Lean中机械化扩展框架与主要可靠性定理。

Conclusion: 模上的加权编程配合恰当条件可安全使用wp推理，统一覆盖概率与非概率/博弈场景；机械化结果给出含可数非确定MDP最小期望奖励不动点刻画的首个机器验证证明。

Abstract: Weighted programming is a framework by Batz et al. for describing mathematical models or optimization problems as imperative programs parametrized over semirings and then analyzing them with program verification techniques like weakest precondition calculi. Applications include reasoning about expected outcomes of probabilistic programs and competitive analysis of online algorithms. We extend weighted programming with (1) countable nondeterminism and (2) user-defined additive rewards, where programs are parametrized over modules instead of semirings. The resulting framework enables reasoning about expected resource consumption of probabilistic programs and about optimal scores of combinatorial games. A technical challenge is that wp-calculi for weighted programs with (1) and (2) over arbitrary modules can be unsound, because it is well-known that continuity properties commonly used in program semantics break in the presence of countable nondeterminism. We study conditions under which a wp-style transformer for reasoning about weighted programs is sound with respect to an operational ground truth defined in terms of weighted decision processes (WDPs) - transition systems that generalize Markov decision processes by drawing transition weights and state rewards from a module. Based on those conditions, we show that a range of concrete instances of the weighted programming framework enjoy sound weakest precondition reasoning. We demonstrate the usefulness of our wp-semantics through case studies for different modules. We mechanized our extended weighted programming framework and our main soundness results in the interactive theorem prover Lean. Instantiating these results for probabilistic programming yields, to the best of our knowledge, the first mechanized proof of a fixed point characterization of minimal expected rewards in Markov decision processes with countable nondeterminism.

</details>
