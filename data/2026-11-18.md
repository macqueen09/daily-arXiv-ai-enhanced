Motivation: 大量高层Petri网形式化方法虽在学术界被研究部署且能提供强保证，但难以进入实际应用：经典Petri网过于简单，高层形式化又过于专用，加之状态空间爆炸，使终端用户难以获得直接收益，因此需要一种折中方案来兼顾Petri网简洁的触发语义与模型检验能力。

Method: 将软件/硬件行为中与实现相关的方面从经典Petri网语义中分离出来，形式化定义带数据感知变迁(data-aware transitions)和托肯的Interpreted Petri Nets；以机器人系统的执行层为建模范例；提供一个轻量工具对经典Petri网进行增强以支持通用应用，并实现数据感知的状态空间探索用于模型检验。

Result: 在自主机器人场景中，通过完整的工业检测任务用例进行展示，对比了经典Petri网与新的数据感知Petri网语义下的仿真与模型检验结果。

Conclusion: 数据感知的解释型Petri网可作为经典与高层形式化之间的折中，保留简洁触发语义与模型检验优势的同时增强表达能力以更贴近被建模系统，且在自主机器人应用中具备实用价值。

Abstract: A large amount of high-level Petri net formalisms has been investigated and deployed in academic settings over the past decades, but these deployments fail to reach practical applications, despite the strong guarantees they can bring to stakeholders. In particular, the lack of direct benefits from end-users reduces the appeal of Petri nets, both due to the overly simple classical Petri net formalism, and the overly specialized high-level formalisms, on top of state-space explosion issues.
  In this work, we investigate how a middle-ground could be reached, by separating the implementation-specific aspects of software or hardware behaviors, from the classical Petri net semantics. The goal is to benefit from the sought-after aspects of Petri nets, namely their simple firing semantic and model-checking capabilities, while still being able to augment their behavior to best reflect the modelled system. We present a formal model of Interpreted Petri nets with data-aware transitions and tokens. We illustrate this formalism by modelling the executive layer of a robotic system, and provide a lightweight tool to augment classical Petri nets for general purpose applications, as well as data-aware state-space exploration for model-checking. Their specialization in the context of autonomous robotics is showcased, with a complete industrial inspection mission use-case, to compare simulation and model-checking results between the classical and the new data-aware Petri net semantic.
