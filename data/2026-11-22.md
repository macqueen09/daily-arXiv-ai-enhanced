TL;DR: 在β-正规η-长型固有类型项框架内，不用完备性定理给出简单类型λ演算可定义同构即遗传置换的新证明，并进一步建立相干定理：同构群的表示等价于一组显式方程，全部在Agda中无公设形式化。

Motivation: 经典结果认为简单类型λ演算的可定义同构恰为遗传置换，由 A→B→C ≅ B→A→C 与同余生成；本文旨在给出更构造性、内部于正规长型的证明，并将其提升为同构群的相干表示，作为 University Algebra 句法完备性猜想的案例研究。

Method: 新证明停留在β-正规η-长型固有类型项中，不使用完备性定理，通过同时下降同构两个分量的归纳法证明；随后在更高层建立相干定理，用群胚律、函子性、对合、自然性与辫子方程组成显式方程列表刻画派生同构的相等性（遗传版对称群Coxeter表示）。全部在Agda中基于带遗传替换的固有类型语法形式化，无公设。

Result: 给出遗传置换结果的新证明；证明两个同构派生表示同一同构当且仅当模所列方程相等，从而这些方程构成了同构群胚的表示，即对称群的遗传版Coxeter表示。

Conclusion: 结果均为构造性且已机器验证，是 University Algebra 句法完备性猜想的案例研究（其语义对应仍开放），并说明了方法的可迁移部分。

Abstract: It is a classical result that the definable isomorphisms of the simply typed lambda-calculus with function types are exactly the hereditary permutations, and that they are generated by $A \to B \to C \cong B \to A \to C$ and congruence. We give a new proof of this result that stays inside beta-normal eta-long typed terms and uses no completeness theorem: an induction that descends through both components of an isomorphism at once. We then go one level up and prove a coherence theorem: two derivations of an isomorphism denote the same isomorphism if and only if they are equal modulo an explicit list of equations (groupoid laws, functoriality, involution, naturality and the braid equation), so these equations present the groupoid of isomorphisms, a hereditary version of the Coxeter presentation of the symmetric groups. All results are formalised in Agda, on intrinsically typed syntax with hereditary substitution, without postulates. The work is a case study for the syntactic completeness conjecture of University Algebra, a proposed solution of Tarski's high school algebra problem using dependent types, whose semantic counterpart is open. We explain the motivation and which parts of the method we expect to carry over.
